\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Nets and Surface Area (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Net:} A 2D pattern that can be folded to form a 3D solid. \\
2. \textbf{Surface Area:} The sum of the areas of all the faces of a 3D shape. \\
- **Rectangular Prism:** Has 6 rectangular faces. $SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)$. \\
- **Triangular Prism:** Has 2 triangular bases and 3 rectangular faces. \\
- **Pyramid (Square-based):** Has 1 square base and 4 triangular faces.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}

\medskip
\noindent
Find the surface area of a square pyramid where the square base has side lengths of 5 cm, and the triangles have a height (slant height) of 6 cm.\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Base Area = $5 \times 5 = 25\text{ cm}^2$.\\
2) Four Triangles Area = $4 \times \left(\frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 6\right) = 4 \times 15 = 60\text{ cm}^2$.\\
3) Total Surface Area = $25 + 60 = 85\text{ cm}^2$.
\end{tcolorbox}
