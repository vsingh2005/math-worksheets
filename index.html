Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kids STEM Studio?

Kids STEM Studio (KSS) provides hands-on, interactive programs in coding, game design, robotics, engineering, and math, designed to nurture a kid's natural curiosity and build computational thinking skills.

What age groups do you serve?

We specialize in enrichment classes for students ranging from ages 4 to 14, introducing foundational science and mathematics concepts in a playful, supportive, and engaging environment.

How does the placement test help my child?

This placement portal assesses your child's math capabilities across grades 6, 7, and 8 to identify their strengths and recommend the perfect math level matching their skill profile.

What classes are available?

We offer afterschool enrichment, weekly classes, summer camps, school break field trips, and birthday parties, covering robotics, Minecraft engineering, coding, and mathematical thinking.
