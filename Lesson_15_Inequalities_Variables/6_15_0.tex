    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Inequalities and Relationships (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Inequality:} A mathematical statement showing that two quantities are not equal ($<$, $>$, $\le$, $\ge$). \\
- An inequality like $x > c$ represents infinitely many solutions. \\
- **Graphing:** Use an open circle for $<$ or $>$, and a closed circle for $\le$ or $\ge$. \\
2. \textbf{Variables:} \\
- **Independent Variable:** The cause or input variable (usually $x$). \\
- **Dependent Variable:** The effect or output variable (usually $y$), which depends on $x$.

\vspace{4mm}
