\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
Arithmetic patterns are repeating sequences of numbers that follow a rule.
\begin{itemize}
  \item **Addition pattern:** $3, 6, 9, 12 \dots$ (Rule: add 3)
  \item **Multiplication pattern:** Even numbers appear in rows of 2, 4, 6, 8, etc.
\end{itemize}
