\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
To multiply a single-digit number by a multiple of 10 (like 20, 30, 40):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Multiply the single digit by the digit in the tens place.
  \item Put a **0** at the end of the product!
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{3mm}
\textbf{Example:}
\[
3 \times 40 = \textsf{?}
\]
Multiply $3 \times 4 = 12$. 
Add the zero to make **120**.
