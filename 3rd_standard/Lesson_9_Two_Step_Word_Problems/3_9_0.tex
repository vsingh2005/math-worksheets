\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
To solve two-step word problems:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Read the problem carefully.
    \item Find the **first step** and solve it.
    \item Use that answer to solve the **second step**.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{3mm}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}
\\
Leo has 3 bags of marbles. Each bag has 6 marbles. He loses 4 marbles in the grass. How many marbles does he have now?
\\
\textbf{Step 1 (Find total marbles):} $3 \times 6 = 18$ marbles.
\\
\textbf{Step 2 (Subtract lost marbles):} $18 - 4 = 14$ marbles.
\\
\textbf{Answer:} Leo has 14 marbles.
\end{tcolorbox}
