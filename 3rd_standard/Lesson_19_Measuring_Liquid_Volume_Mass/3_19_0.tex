\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
\begin{itemize}
    \item **Liquid Volume** is measured in **Liters (L)**.
    \item **Mass (weight)** is measured in **Grams (g)** for light items and **Kilograms (kg)** for heavy items ($1\text{ kg} = 1000\text{ g}$).
\end{itemize}
