\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
Multiplication and division are **opposites** (inverse operations). 
They form **fact families**. For example, 3, 4, and 12 make:
\[
3 \times 4 = 12 \qquad 4 \times 3 = 12
\]
\[
12 \div 3 = 4 \qquad 12 \div 4 = 3
\]
