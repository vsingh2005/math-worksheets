\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
\textbf{Commutative Property:} You can multiply numbers in any order, and the answer is the same!
\[
3 \times 4 = 12 \quad \text{and} \quad 4 \times 3 = 12
\]

\textbf{Associative Property:} You can group numbers in different ways to multiply them.
\[
(2 \times 3) \times 4 = 6 \times 4 = 24
\]
\[
2 \times (3 \times 4) = 2 \times 12 = 24
\]
