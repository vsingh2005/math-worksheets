\noindent
Write equations and solve to answer each question.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A company has \$500 to buy office chairs. Each chair costs \$42.50. \\
\textbf{a)} Write a multiplication equation representing the number of chairs $n$ they can buy. \blanklong \\
\textbf{b)} Solve the equation. What does the fractional part of your answer mean?

\vspace{2cm}

\item A triangle has a base of 8 inches and an area of 36 square inches. Use the formula $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$ to set up an equation, and solve for the height $h$.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A rectangular garden is enclosed by 60 feet of fencing. If the length is 18 feet, write an equation to find the width $w$ ($2 \times \text{length} + 2 \times \text{width} = 60$) and solve it.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}
