\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
You can find the area of a rectangle by multiplying its **length** times its **width**:
\[
\text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width}
\]
We can also split (decompose) larger rectangles into smaller ones to make them easier to multiply.
\[
\text{Area of a } 6 \times 7 \text{ rectangle} = (6 \times 5) + (6 \times 2) = 30 + 12 = 42
\]
