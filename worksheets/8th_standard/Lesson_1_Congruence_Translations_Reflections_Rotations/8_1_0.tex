% ==========================================================
% SECTION 1: WHAT ARE RIGID MOTIONS?
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Foundation: Translations, Reflections \& Rotations}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
A \textbf{rigid motion} is a transformation that changes the position of a figure but preserves its size and shape (distance and angle measures). The original figure is the \textit{pre-image}, and the new figure is the \textit{image} (often denoted with a prime symbol, like $A'$).

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Translation:} A ``slide'' horizontally, vertically, or both. $(x, y) \rightarrow (x+a, y+b)$.
    \item \textbf{Reflection:} A ``flip'' over a line of symmetry. 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Across $x$-axis: $(x, y) \rightarrow (x, -y)$
        \item Across $y$-axis: $(x, y) \rightarrow (-x, y)$
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Rotation:} A ``turn'' around a center point (e.g., origin).
    \begin{itemize}
        \item $90^\circ$ clockwise: $(x, y) \rightarrow (y, -x)$
        \item $180^\circ$ rotation: $(x, y) \rightarrow (-x, -y)$
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Applying Transformations}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Let's look at how coordinates change when we apply transformations.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]

\textbf{Worked Example 1: Translation and Reflection}

\medskip

\textbf{Problem:} A point $P(-4, 5)$ is translated right 6 units and down 2 units to become $P'$. It is then reflected across the $x$-axis to become $P''$. Find the coordinates of $P''$.

\medskip

\textbf{Solution:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1 (Translation):} Right 6 means $x + 6$. Down 2 means $y - 2$.
    \item $P' = (-4 + 6, 5 - 2) = (2, 3)$.
    \item \textbf{Step 2 (Reflection):} Across the $x$-axis means we negate the $y$-value: $(x, y) \rightarrow (x, -y)$.
    \item $P'' = (2, -3)$.
\end{itemize}

\end{tcolorbox}
