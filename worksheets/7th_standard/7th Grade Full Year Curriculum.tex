% ── DOMAIN 1: Ratios & Proportional Relationships ────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainA}\textbf{Domain: Ratios & Proportional Relationships}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
1 & Ratios & Proportional Relationships & Unit 1: Rates & Proportions & Unit Rates with Fractions &
Compute unit rates associated with ratios of fractions, including ratios of lengths and areas measured in like or different units (e.g., miles per hour, price per ounce with fractional quantities). &
7.RP.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
2 & Ratios & Proportional Relationships & Unit 1: Rates & Proportions & Proportional Relationships &
Decide whether two quantities are in a proportional relationship using tables and graphs. Identify the constant of proportionality in tables, graphs, equations, diagrams, and verbal descriptions. &
7.RP.A.2a / 7.RP.A.2b \\

\rowcolor{row1}
3 & Ratios & Proportional Relationships & Unit 1: Rates & Proportions & Equations of Proportional Relationships &
Represent proportional relationships with equations of the form $y = kx$. Explain what points on the graph mean, with special attention to $(0, 0)$ and $(1, r)$ where $r$ is the unit rate. &
7.RP.A.2c / 7.RP.A.2d \\

\rowcolor{row2}
4 & Ratios & Proportional Relationships & Unit 2: Percents & Applications & Percents: Increase, Decrease & Error &
Use proportional relationships to solve multi-step percent problems including markups, markdowns, discounts, commissions, fees, percent increase and decrease, and percent error. &
7.RP.A.3 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
5 & Ratios & Proportional Relationships & Unit 2: Percents & Applications & Financial Literacy: Tax, Tip & Interest &
Apply percent and proportional reasoning to financial contexts: calculate sales tax, gratuities, commissions, markups, markdowns, and simple interest ($I = Prt$). &
7.RP.A.3 (applied) \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 2: The Number System ──────────────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainB}\textbf{Domain: The Number System}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
6 & The Number System & Unit 3: Rational Number Operations & Adding & Subtracting Rational Numbers &
Apply and extend understanding of addition and subtraction to rational numbers. Represent on a number line; interpret sums and differences in real-world contexts (temperature, elevation, debt). &
7.NS.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
7 & The Number System & Unit 3: Rational Number Operations & Multiplying & Dividing Rational Numbers &
Apply and extend understanding of multiplication and division to rational numbers. Understand products and quotients of signed numbers; interpret rules for multiplying and dividing integers. &
7.NS.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
8 & The Number System & Unit 3: Rational Number Operations & Four Operations with Rational Numbers &
Solve real-world and mathematical problems involving all four operations with rational numbers, including complex fractions and multi-step situations. &
7.NS.A.3 \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 3: Expressions & Equations ────────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainC}\textbf{Domain: Expressions & Equations}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
9 & Expressions & Equations & Unit 4: Algebraic Expressions & Linear Expressions with Rational Coefficients &
Apply properties of operations to add, subtract, factor, and expand linear expressions with rational coefficients. Understand how rewriting expressions reveals relationships (e.g., $a + 0.05a = 1.05a$). &
7.EE.A.1 / 7.EE.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
10 & Expressions & Equations & Unit 5: Equations & Inequalities & Solving Two-Step Equations &
Solve word problems leading to equations of the form $px + q = r$ and $p(x + q) = r$. Fluently solve these equations and compare algebraic vs.\ arithmetic approaches. &
7.EE.B.3 / 7.EE.B.4a \\

\rowcolor{row1}
11 & Expressions & Equations & Unit 5: Equations & Inequalities & Solving Two-Step Inequalities &
Solve word problems leading to inequalities of the form $px + q > r$ or $px + q < r$. Graph the solution set on a number line and interpret in context. &
7.EE.B.4b \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 4: Geometry ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainD}\textbf{Domain: Geometry}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
12 & Geometry & Unit 6: Scale & Construction & Scale Drawings &
Solve problems involving scale drawings of geometric figures, including computing actual lengths and areas from a scale drawing and reproducing a drawing at a different scale. &
7.G.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
13 & Geometry & Unit 6: Scale & Construction & Drawing Geometric Figures &
Draw geometric shapes with given conditions (triangles from three measures of angles or sides). Understand when conditions determine a unique triangle, more than one, or none. &
7.G.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
14 & Geometry & Unit 6: Scale & Construction & Slicing 3D Figures &
Describe the two-dimensional figures that result from slicing three-dimensional figures (right rectangular prisms, right rectangular pyramids) parallel or perpendicular to the base. &
7.G.A.3 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
15 & Geometry & Unit 7: Measurement & Angles & Circles: Circumference & Area &
Know and use the formulas for circumference ($C = \pi d$) and area ($A = \pi r^2$) of a circle. Solve real-world and mathematical problems involving circles. &
7.G.B.4 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
16 & Geometry & Unit 7: Measurement & Angles & Angle Relationships &
Use facts about supplementary, complementary, vertical, and adjacent angles to set up and solve simple equations for an unknown angle in a figure. &
7.G.B.5 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
17 & Geometry & Unit 7: Measurement & Angles & Area & Surface Area of Composite Figures &
Solve real-world problems involving area, surface area, and volume of figures composed of triangles, quadrilaterals, polygons, cubes, and right prisms. &
7.G.B.6 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
18 & Geometry & Unit 7: Measurement & Angles & Volume of Prisms &
Find volume of right prisms with triangular, trapezoidal, and other polygonal bases. Connect to decomposing 3D figures and apply to real-world packing and capacity problems. &
7.G.B.6 (applied) \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 5: Statistics & Probability ───────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainE}\textbf{Domain: Statistics & Probability}} \\
\midrule 

\rowcolor{row1}
19 & Statistics & Probability & Unit 8: Sampling & Inference & Statistical Sampling & Inference &
Understand that statistics can be used to gain information about a population by examining a sample. Use data from a random sample to draw inferences and gauge variation in estimates. &
7.SP.A.1 / 7.SP.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
20 & Statistics & Probability & Unit 8: Sampling & Inference & Comparing Two Populations &
Draw informal comparative inferences about two populations. Compare two sets of data using measures of center and variability; understand what a greater spread or different median means. &
7.SP.B.3 / 7.SP.B.4 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
21 & Statistics & Probability & Unit 9: Probability & Introduction to Probability &
Understand that the probability of a chance event is a number between 0 and 1. Approximate probability through experiments; compare experimental to theoretical probability. &
7.SP.C.5 / 7.SP.C.6 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
22 & Statistics & Probability & Unit 9: Probability & Probability Models &
Develop uniform and non-uniform probability models. Use models to find probabilities of events; compare predictions with observed frequencies and explain discrepancies. &
7.SP.C.7 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
23 & Statistics & Probability & Unit 9: Probability & Compound Events & Sample Spaces &
Find probabilities of compound events using organized lists, tables, tree diagrams, and simulation. Understand that the probability of a compound event is the fraction of outcomes in the sample space. &
7.SP.C.8 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
24 & Statistics & Probability & Unit 10: Capstone & Multi-Step Ratio & Geometry Word Problems &
Solve complex multi-step word problems combining proportional reasoning, percent, and geometry. Use scale drawings, unit rates, and area/volume formulas in integrated real-world contexts. &
7.RP.A.1--3 / 7.G.A.1 / 7.G.B.4--6 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
25 & Statistics & Probability & Unit 10: Capstone & Cumulative Review & Real-World Math &
Solve multi-step real-world problems drawing on all 7th grade domains: rational numbers, proportional relationships, expressions, geometry, and probability. Communicate mathematical reasoning clearly. &
7.MP.1--7.MP.8 \\
