\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=5.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item The population of a town increased from $12,500$ to $14,375$ residents over a five-year period. What is the percent increase in the town's population?
\item A video game console originally priced at $\$350$ is on sale for $\$280$. What is the percent decrease in the price of the console?
\item Last year, Maya ran the $100$-meter dash in $15.0$ seconds. This year, she reduced her running time to $13.8$ seconds. What is the percent decrease in her running time?
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=5.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]
\item A local bookstore bought a rare novel for $\$45$ and marked up the price by $60\%$ to sell it in their store. What is the retail selling price of the novel?
\item A real estate agent earns a $2.5\%$ commission on the sale of a home. If the agent sells a house for $\$320,000$, how much commission does the agent earn?
\item A restaurant bill comes to $\$65.00$ before tax and tip. 
\end{enumerate}

\item An electronics store is offering a $15\%$ discount on a laptop that normally costs $\$850$. A sales tax of $6\%$ is applied to the discounted price. What is the final cost of the laptop?

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part D: Percent Increase \& Decrease}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=5.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]
\item A student estimates that the length of a wooden board is $1.5$ meters. The actual measured length of the board is $1.62$ meters. What is the student's percent error?
\item A meteorologist predicted that a city would receive $4.5$ inches of snow during a winter storm. The actual snowfall recorded was $5.2$ inches. Find the percent error of the meteorologist's prediction.
\item A contractor estimated that it would take $75$ hours to complete a home renovation project. The project actually took $90$ hours to complete. What was the percent error of the contractor's estimate?
\end{enumerate}
