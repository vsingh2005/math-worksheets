\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Powers of 10 and Exponents}}
\end{tcolorbox}
\vspace{3mm}
Exponents represent repeated multiplication:
\[
10^1 = 10, \quad 10^2 = 100, \quad 10^3 = 1,000
\]
Multiplying by $10^2$ shifts digits 2 places to the left:
\[
4.5 \times 10^2 = 450
\]
