\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}
\begin{itemize}
    \item **Factors** are numbers you multiply together to get another number.
    \item **Multiples** are the products of multiplying a number by other whole numbers.
    \item **Prime numbers** have exactly two factors: 1 and themselves.
    \item **Composite numbers** have more than two factors.
\end{itemize}
