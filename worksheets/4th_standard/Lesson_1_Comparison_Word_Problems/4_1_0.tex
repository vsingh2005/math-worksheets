    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Introduction: Multiplicative Comparisons}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
A **multiplicative comparison** compares two quantities by showing that one quantity is a certain number of times larger than the other.
\begin{itemize}
    \item **Addition comparison:** "Sara has 5 apples and John has 3 more than Sara." ($5 + 3 = 8$)
    \item **Multiplicative comparison:** "Sara has 5 apples and John has 3 times as many as Sara." ($5 \times 3 = 15$)
\end{itemize}

\vspace{3mm}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm]
\textbf{Tape Diagram Representation:}
\\
Sara's apples: \tikz[baseline=-0.5ex]{\draw[thick, fill=blue!20] (0,0) rectangle (1,0.4); \node at (0.5,0.2) {5};}
\\
John's apples: \tikz[baseline=-0.5ex]{
  \draw[thick, fill=blue!20] (0,0) rectangle (1,0.4); \node at (0.5,0.2) {5};
  \draw[thick, fill=blue!20] (1,0) rectangle (2,0.4); \node at (1.5,0.2) {5};
  \draw[thick, fill=blue!20] (2,0) rectangle (3,0.4); \node at (2.5,0.2) {5};
}
\\ \\
Equation: $3 \times 5 = 15$. John has 15 apples.
\end{tcolorbox}
