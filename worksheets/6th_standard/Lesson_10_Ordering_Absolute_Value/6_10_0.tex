% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Comparing, Ordering, and Absolute Value (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Comparing:} On a number line, values increase from left to right. $-5 < -2$ because $-5$ is to the left of $-2$. \\ \vspace{0.5cm} \\
2. \textbf{Absolute Value:} The distance of a number from 0 on a number line, denoted as $|x|$. Absolute value is always positive or zero. Example: $|-5| = 5$, and $|5| = 5$. \\ \vspace{0.5cm} \\
3. \textbf{Order vs. Absolute Value:} A number with a smaller value can have a larger absolute value. For instance, $-10 < -2$, but $|-10| > |-2|$ (meaning a debt of \$10 is larger than a debt of \$2).

\vspace{0.5cm} \\

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 1: Comparing Balances}
\vspace{0.3cm} \\
\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Problem:} \\ Account A has a balance of $-\$40$, and Account B has $-\$15$. Compare their values using an inequality, and explain who has more debt.
\vspace{1cm} \\
\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Compare the values: $-40 < -15$. \\ \vspace{1cm} \\
2) Compare the absolute values to find the magnitude of debt: $|-40| = 40$ and $|-15| = 15$. Since $40 > 15$, Account A has a larger absolute debt. \\ \vspace{1cm} \\
3) Answer: Account A has a lower balance ($-40 < -15$), which means they owe more money (\$40 debt vs. \$15 debt). \\
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
% SECTION 2: OPPOSITES AND DISTANCE
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Opposites and Distance on the Number Line}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Opposites:} Two numbers that are the same distance from 0 but on opposite sides. The opposite of $x$ is $-x$. The opposite of a negative number is positive: $-(-x) = x$. \\
2. \textbf{Distance:} Absolute value tells us the distance between a number and 0. To find the distance between a positive number and a negative number, we add their absolute values.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=4mm, bottom=4mm]
\vspace{0.5cm}
\textbf{Core Concept: Outside Negative Signs with Absolute Value}
\vspace{0.5cm}

\medskip
An absolute value expression acts like parentheses; you must evaluate the inside first! A negative sign outside the bars means \textbf{"the opposite of the absolute value."}

\begin{itemize}[itemsep=6pt]
    \item \textbf{Example 1:} Evaluate $|-12|$. 
    \\ The distance from $-12$ to $0$ is $12$. So, $|-12| = \mathbf{12}$.
    
    \item \textbf{Example 2:} Evaluate $-|-12|$.
    \\ First, evaluate the inside: $|-12| = 12$. 
    \\ Next, apply the outside negative sign (the opposite): $\mathbf{-12}$.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.5cm}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]

\textbf{Worked Example 2: Distance Between Elevations}
\vspace{0.5cm}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Problem:} A bird flies at 25 feet above sea level ($+25$), and a fish swims at 10 feet below sea level ($-10$). What is the distance between them?

\vspace{0.5cm}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Find the absolute values of each position: $|25| = 25$ and $|-10| = 10$. \\
2) Since they are on opposite sides of sea level (0), add the absolute values to find the total distance: $25 + 10 = 35$ feet. \\
3) Answer: The distance between the bird and the fish is \textbf{35 feet}.
\end{tcolorbox}
