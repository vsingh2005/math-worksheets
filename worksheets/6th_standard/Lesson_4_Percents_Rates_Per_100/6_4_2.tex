\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Quick Review: Percents}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Percent = a ratio out of 100. Three types of problems: \\
(1) Find a percent \textbf{of} a number (multiply), \\
(2) Find the \textbf{whole} given part and percent (divide), \\
(3) Find what \textbf{percent} one is of another (divide).

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
% PART A — FINDING THE WHOLE
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Find the Whole (Given Part \& Percent)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Divide the part by the percent (as a decimal). Show your work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.8cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item 15 is 30\% of what number?

\vspace{1.3cm}

\item 24 is 50\% of what number?

\vspace{1.3cm}

\item 8 is 20\% of what number?

\vspace{1.3cm}

\item 45 is 75\% of what number?

\vspace{1.3cm}

\item 12 is 25\% of what number?

\end{enumerate}

\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Real-World Scenarios}}

\noindent

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A school has 600 students. 45\% are sixth graders. How many sixth graders are there?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item A discount store is selling shirts for 60\% off the original price. If the original price was \$40, what is the sale price? (Hint: If 60\% off, the sale price is 40\% of original)

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item Out of 150 students surveyed, 90 prefer chocolate ice cream. What percent prefer chocolate?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\vspace{1.8cm}

\end{enumerate}
