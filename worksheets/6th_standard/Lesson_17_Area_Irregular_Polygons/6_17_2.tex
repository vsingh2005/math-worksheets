\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Developing: Composite Shapes and Subtraction Method}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Apply decomposition and subtraction strategies to find areas of composite and irregular figures.

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
% PART A — COMPOSITE SHAPES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Composite Shapes}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Find the area of each composite shape. Show all work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A shape consists of a rectangle of $10\text{ cm} \times 6\text{ cm}$ and a right triangle with a base of 6 cm and height of 4 cm attached to its shorter side. Find the total area.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A trapezoid with parallel sides of 8 m and 14 m, and a height of 6 m. Decompose it into a rectangle and two triangles to find its area.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A composite figure has a rectangular section ($12\text{ ft} \times 5\text{ ft}$) and a parallelogram ($\text{base} = 12\text{ ft}$, $\text{height} = 4\text{ ft}$) attached on top. Find the total area.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART B — SUBTRACTION METHOD
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Subtraction Method}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Find the area of the shaded region by subtracting the removed section from the whole. Show all steps.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.9cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A rectangular card is $15\text{ cm} \times 10\text{ cm}$. A square of side length 4 cm is cut out of the center. Find the remaining area.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A pool deck has outer dimensions $20\text{ ft} \times 30\text{ ft}$. The swimming pool inside is $12\text{ ft} \times 24\text{ ft}$. Find the deck area.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A rectangular banner is $6\text{ m} \times 4\text{ m}$. A triangular notch ($\text{base} = 2\text{ m}$, $\text{height} = 1\text{ m}$) is cut from the center of the bottom edge. Find the remaining area.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART C — AREA FROM COORDINATES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Coordinate Polygon Area}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Find the area of each polygon from its coordinates. Show your decomposition strategy.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.3cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item Vertices: $(0, 0)$, $(8, 0)$, $(8, 5)$, $(4, 5)$, $(4, 8)$, $(0, 8)$. Decompose into two rectangles and find the total area.

\vspace{6cm}

\item Vertices: $(-3, -2)$, $(5, -2)$, $(5, 4)$, $(1, 4)$, $(1, 1)$, $(-3, 1)$. Find the total area by decomposing into two rectangles.

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART D — CHALLENGE PROBLEMS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Challenge: Multi-Step Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each multi-step problem. Show all calculations.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.4cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A kitchen floor is an irregular polygon composed of a rectangle ($12\text{ ft} \times 10\text{ ft}$) plus a triangular corner addition ($\text{base} = 5\text{ ft}$, $\text{height} = 4\text{ ft}$). If tile costs \$3.75 per square foot, what is the total cost to tile the floor?

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A school field has an outer rectangle of $80\text{ m} \times 50\text{ m}$. A rectangular building ($20\text{ m} \times 15\text{ m}$) occupies a corner, and a triangular playground ($\text{base} = 10\text{ m}$, $\text{height} = 20\text{ m}$) is located in the center. Find the remaining open area of the field.

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A decorative wall panel is composed of a $3\text{ ft} \times 6\text{ ft}$ rectangle with four identical square cutouts ($0.5\text{ ft} \times 0.5\text{ ft}$ each) evenly spaced. Find the total area of the solid panel remaining.

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}
