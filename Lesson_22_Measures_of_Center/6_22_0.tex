% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Mean, Median, Mode, and Range (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Mean (Average):} Sum of the data values divided by the number of values. Represents the ``fair share'' or balance point. \\
2. \textbf{Median:} The middle number when values are sorted in order. If there is an even number of values, it is the average of the two middle numbers. \\
3. \textbf{Mode:} The value(s) that appear most frequently. \\
4. \textbf{Range:} The difference between the maximum and minimum values (measures spread). \\
5. \textbf{Outliers:} Values that are much larger or smaller than the rest of the data. Outliers affect the mean significantly, but have little effect on the median.

\vspace{4mm}
