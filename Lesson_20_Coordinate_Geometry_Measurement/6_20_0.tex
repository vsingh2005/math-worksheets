\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Coordinate Geometry and Unit Conversions (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Coordinate Geometry:} Draw polygons on a coordinate plane by plotting their vertices. Find side lengths by subtracting coordinates of points sharing an axis. \\
2. \textbf{Measurement Conversions:} Use ratio reasoning to convert units. \\
- \textbf{Customary:} 1 foot = 12 inches, 1 yard = 3 feet, 1 mile = 5280 feet. \\
- \textbf{Metric:} 1 meter = 100 centimeters, 1 kilometer = 1000 meters.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}

\medskip
\noindent
Find the perimeter of a rectangle with vertices at $(1, 2)$ and $(5, 2)$ on a horizontal side. If each unit is 3 inches, find the length of this side in inches.\\
\textbf{Solution:}
$$\text{Grid Distance} = 5 - 1 = 4\text{ units.}$$
$$\text{Actual Length} = 4\text{ units} \times 3\text{ inches/unit} = 12\text{ inches.}$$
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}
