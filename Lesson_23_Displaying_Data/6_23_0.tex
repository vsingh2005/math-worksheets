\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Histograms and Box-and-Whisker Plots (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Histogram:} A bar graph where data is grouped into intervals (bins) of equal size. The height of each bar represents the frequency of data in that interval. Bins must not overlap. \\
2. \textbf{Box Plot (Box-and-Whisker):} Summarizes data using a 5-number summary: \\
- **Minimum:** The smallest value. \\
- **Lower Quartile ($Q_1$):** The median of the lower half of data. \\
- **Median ($Q_2$):** The middle value of the data. \\
- **Upper Quartile ($Q_3$):** The median of the upper half of data. \\
- **Maximum:** The largest value. \\
3. \textbf{Interquartile Range (IQR):} $Q_3 - Q_1$ (represents the middle $50\%$ of the data).

\vspace{4mm}
