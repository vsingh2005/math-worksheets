\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Foundation: Angle Relationships \& Parallel Lines}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
When two parallel lines are intersected by a third line (called a \textbf{transversal}), special angle relationships are created. Understanding these relationships allows us to solve for unknown angles.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Corresponding Angles:} Angles in the same relative position at each intersection where the straight line crosses the parallel lines. They are \textbf{equal}.
    \item \textbf{Alternate Interior Angles:} Angles on opposite sides of the transversal and between the parallel lines. They are \textbf{equal}.
    \item \textbf{Alternate Exterior Angles:} Angles on opposite sides of the transversal and outside the parallel lines. They are \textbf{equal}.
    \item \textbf{Same-Side Interior Angles:} Angles on the same side of the transversal and between the parallel lines. They add up to $180^\circ$ (\textbf{supplementary}).
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4mm}

\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Applying Angle Relationships}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
We can use algebraic expressions to solve for missing angles.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]

\textbf{Worked Example 1: Finding Missing Variables}

\medskip

\textbf{Problem:} Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. The alternate interior angles are given as $(3x + 10)^\circ$ and $(5x - 20)^\circ$. Find the value of $x$ and the measure of the angles.

\medskip

\textbf{Solution:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1 (Identify Relationship):} Alternate interior angles are equal.
    \item \textbf{Step 2 (Set up equation):} $3x + 10 = 5x - 20$.
    \item \textbf{Step 3 (Solve for $x$):} 
    \begin{align*}
        10 &= 2x - 20 \\
        30 &= 2x \\
        15 &= x
    \end{align*}
    \item \textbf{Step 4 (Find the angle):} Plug $x=15$ back into either expression. $3(15) + 10 = 45 + 10 = 55^\circ$.
\end{itemize}

\end{tcolorbox}
