\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Foundation: Similar Figures}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Two figures are \textbf{similar} if one can be obtained from the other by a sequence of rigid motions (translations, reflections, rotations) and a \textbf{dilation}.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Angles:} Corresponding angles in similar figures are \textbf{congruent} (exactly the same measure).
    \item \textbf{Side Lengths:} Corresponding side lengths in similar figures are \textbf{proportional}. They have the same ratio (the scale factor).
    \item \textbf{Symbol:} The symbol for similarity is $\sim$. If Triangle $ABC$ is similar to Triangle $DEF$, we write $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4mm}

\textbf{Visual Representation: Similar Triangles}

\medskip

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.8]
% Small Triangle
\draw[thick, blue] (0,0) -- (3,0) node[midway, below] {$3$} -- (0,4) node[midway, left] {$4$} -- cycle;
\node[blue] at (1.5, 2.5) {$5$};
\node[blue] at (1, -1) {Small Triangle};

% Big Triangle
\draw[thick, red] (5,0) -- (11,0) node[midway, below] {$6$} -- (5,8) node[midway, left] {$8$} -- cycle;
\node[red] at (8, 4.5) {$10$};
\node[red] at (7, -1) {Large Triangle};

% Angle markers
\draw (0.4, 0) arc (0:90:0.4);
\draw (5.4, 0) arc (0:90:0.4);

\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\medskip
Notice that the angles are the same (e.g., the $90^\circ$ angle), but the side lengths of the large triangle are exactly twice the side lengths of the small triangle. The ratio of corresponding sides is $\dfrac{6}{3} = \dfrac{8}{4} = \dfrac{10}{5} = 2$.

\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Solving for Missing Sides}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Because corresponding sides are proportional, we can set up equivalent ratios (proportions) to solve for missing side lengths.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]

\textbf{Worked Example 1: Using Proportions}

\medskip

\textbf{Problem:} $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$. Side $AB = 4$, $BC = 6$. Side $DE = 10$, and $EF = x$. Find $x$.

\medskip

\textbf{Solution:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1 (Identify Corresponding Sides):} $AB$ corresponds to $DE$. $BC$ corresponds to $EF$.
    \item \textbf{Step 2 (Set up Proportion):} $\dfrac{\text{Small Side 1}}{\text{Large Side 1}} = \dfrac{\text{Small Side 2}}{\text{Large Side 2}}$
    \item $\dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{6}{x}$
    \item \textbf{Step 3 (Cross-Multiply):} $4 \cdot x = 10 \cdot 6$
    \item $4x = 60$
    \item $x = 15$
\end{itemize}

\end{tcolorbox}
