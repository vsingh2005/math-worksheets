\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Beginning: Linear Equations and Systems Fundamentals}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{5mm}

\noindent Welcome to your first worksheet on Linear Equations and Systems! This worksheet will help you review the basic concepts of solving linear equations in one variable and introduce you to the idea of linear systems. Remember, a linear equation is an equation that forms a straight line when graphed. Our goal is often to find the value(s) of the variable(s) that make the equation true.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm, itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Equation:} A mathematical statement that two expressions are equal.
    \item \textbf{Variable:} A symbol (usually a letter) representing an unknown value.
    \item \textbf{Solution:} The value(s) of the variable(s) that make the equation true.
    \item \textbf{Inverse Operations:} Operations that undo each other (e.g., addition and subtraction, multiplication and division). We use them to isolate the variable.
\end{itemize}
