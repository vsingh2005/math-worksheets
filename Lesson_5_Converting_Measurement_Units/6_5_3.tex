\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Chain Conversions}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Convert through multiple units to solve. Show all work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item How many inches are in 2 miles? (12 in = 1 ft, 5,280 ft = 1 mi) Show all steps.

\vspace{2cm}

\item How many millimeters are in 3 kilometers? (10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m, 1,000 m = 1 km)

\vspace{2cm}

\item A package weighs 5 pounds. Convert to ounces, then to grams if 1 oz $\approx$ 28 grams. How many grams?

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART B — RATE AND CONVERSION PROBLEMS

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Converting Rates and Speeds}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve problems involving conversions and rates.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A car travels 60 miles per hour. How many feet per hour? (Hint: Convert miles to feet)

\vspace{2cm}

\item Water flows at 1,000 milliliters per minute. How many liters per hour? (1,000 mL = 1 L, 60 minutes = 1 hour)

\vspace{2cm}

\item A runner completes 5 kilometers in 30 minutes. How many meters per minute?

\vspace{2cm}

\item A printer prints 60 inches per second. How many feet per second?

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Apply to Real Problems}}

\noindent
Solve. Show all conversion steps and reasoning.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.3cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A room is 4 yards long and 3 yards wide. What is the perimeter in feet? What is the area in square feet?

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A swimming pool is 50 meters long. How many feet is that? (1 meter $\approx$ 3.3 feet)

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A package of meat weighs 3 pounds 8 ounces (3 lb 8 oz). Convert to total ounces.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A hiking trail is 12 miles long. A hiker travels 3 miles per hour. How many hours does it take? Express your answer in hours and minutes.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Challenge Problems}}

\noindent
Reason through complex unit conversion scenarios.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.4cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\end{enumerate}
