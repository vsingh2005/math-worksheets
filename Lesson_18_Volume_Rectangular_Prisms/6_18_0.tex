\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Volume of Rectangular Prisms (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Volume:} The amount of 3D space a solid shape occupies, measured in cubic units. \\
2. \textbf{Formulas for Rectangular Prisms:} \\
- $Volume = length \times width \times height = l \times w \times h$ \\
- $Volume = Base \ Area \times height = B \times h$ \\
3. \textbf{Fractional Side Lengths:} The same formula applies when side lengths are fractions or decimals. You can also think of packing the prism with small unit cubes (e.g., of side length $\frac{1}{2}$ unit).

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}

\medskip
\noindent
Find the volume of a rectangular prism with length $3\frac{1}{2}$ inches, width 2 inches, and height $1\frac{1}{2}$ inches.\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
$$Volume = l \times w \times h = \frac{7}{2} \times 2 \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{42}{4} = 10\frac{1}{2}\text{ cubic inches.}$$
\end{tcolorbox}
