% ==========================================================
% PART A — PERCENT INCREASE AND DECREASE
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Percent Increase and Decrease}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Find the new amount after the increase or decrease. Show your work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1.8cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A store increases the price of a \$50 item by 20\%. What is the new price?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item A population of 1,000 decreases by 30\%. What is the new population?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item A restaurant bill is \$80. With a 18\% tip, what is the total amount?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item A car costs \$20,000. It depreciates (loses value) by 15\% in one year. What is it worth after one year?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item A student's score improved by 25\% from 60 points. What is the new score?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART B — MULTI-STEP PERCENT PROBLEMS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Multi-Step Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve using multiple percent steps. Show all work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A jacket costs \$100. It is on sale for 40\% off. Then the store adds 8\% sales tax. What is the final price you pay?

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A school has 500 students. 60\% are in the middle school, and of those, 50\% are boys. How many boys are in middle school?

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A store marks up items by 50\% from cost. If an item costs the store \$20, what price does the store charge customers?

\vspace{2.2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART C — COMPARING PERCENTS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Compare Percents and Values}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Compare situations involving percents. Show your work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item Store A: 30\% off a \$50 item.\\
Store B: 40\% off a \$60 item.\\
Which gives you the bigger discount in dollars? By how much?

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item Option 1: Save 20\% on \$200.\\
Option 2: Save 10\% on \$500.\\
Which saves you more money?

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A test has 80 questions. Student A gets 75\% correct. Student B gets 90\% correct but took a harder test with only 60 questions. How many did each student get right? Who performed better?

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART D — CHALLENGE AND REASONING

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Challenge Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve these complex problems. Explain your reasoning.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.3cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item If a price increases by 25\% and then decreases by 25\%, is it back to the original price? Show with an example using a \$100 item.

\vspace{2.5cm}

\item A company has 250 employees. 20\% work part-time and the rest work full-time. Of the part-time workers, 50\% are women. How many part-time women work at the company?

\vspace{2.5cm}

\item Your allowance is \$50. You spend 30\% on books, 40\% on movies, and the rest on snacks. How much do you spend on snacks? What percent is that?

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}
