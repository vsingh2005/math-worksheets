\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Financial Literacy: Budgets, Percents, and Discounts (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Budgeting:} A plan to balance income (money earned) and expenses (money spent). \\
2. \textbf{Percentage Discounts:} To find a discount, convert the percentage to a decimal and multiply by the original price. \\
- $Discount = Price \times Rate$ \\
- $Sale \ Price = Original \ Price - Discount$ \\
3. \textbf{Sales Tax:} An additional percentage charged on top of the sale price. \\
4. \textbf{Simple Interest:} Money earned or paid for borrowing money. $Interest = Principal \times Rate \times Time \ (I = Prt)$.

\vspace{4mm}
