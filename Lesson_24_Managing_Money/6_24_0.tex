\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Financial Literacy: Budgets, Percents, and Discounts (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Budgeting:} A plan to balance income (money earned) and expenses (money spent). \\
2. \textbf{Percentage Discounts:} To find a discount, convert the percentage to a decimal and multiply by the original price. \\
- $\text{Discount} = \text{Price} \times \text{Rate}$ \\
- $\text{Sale Price} = \text{Original Price} - \text{Discount}$ \\
3. \textbf{Sales Tax:} An additional percentage charged on top of the sale price. \\
4. \textbf{Simple Interest:} Money earned or paid for borrowing money. $\text{Interest} = \text{Principal} \times \text{Rate} \times \text{Time}$ ($I = Prt$).

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}

\medskip
\noindent
A book is originally priced at $\$20$. It is on sale for $15\%$ off. If the sales tax is $5\%$, calculate the final price.\\
\textbf{Solution:}\\
1) $\text{Discount} = \$20 \times 0.15 = \$3$.\\
2) $\text{Sale Price} = \$20 - \$3 = \$17$.\\
3) $\text{Tax} = \$17 \times 0.05 = \$0.85$.\\
4) $\text{Final Price} = \$17 + \$0.85 = \$17.85$.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}
