% SECTION 1: EXPONENTS AND THE ORDER OF OPERATIONS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Exponents and the Order of Operations (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Exponential Notation:} $b^n = b \times b \times \dots \times b$ ($n$ times). Here, $b$ is the \textbf{base} and $n$ is the \textbf{exponent}. Example: $2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$. \\
2. \textbf{Order of Operations (PEMDAS/GEMS):} \\
- \textbf{P}arenthesis / Groupings \\
- \textbf{E}xponents \\
- \textbf{M}ultiplication \& \textbf{D}ivision (from left to right) \\
- \textbf{A}ddition \& \textbf{S}ubtraction (from left to right)

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 1:}

\medskip
\noindent
Evaluate the expression: $3 \times 4^2 - (5 + 2 \times 3)$\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Grouping first: $5 + 2 \times 3 = 5 + 6 = 11$. \\
2) Exponents: $4^2 = 16$. \\
3) Multiplications/Divisions: $3 \times 16 = 48$. \\
4) Additions/Subtractions: $48 - 11 = 37$.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}

% SECTION 2: COMPLEX FRACTION EXPRESSIONS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Complex Fraction Expressions and Order of Operations}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Fraction Bars as Grouping Symbols:} A horizontal fraction bar acts as a grouping symbol. You must simplify the entire numerator and the entire denominator before performing the final division. \\
2. \textbf{Parentheses inside Parentheses:} When nested grouping symbols are used, evaluate from the innermost set to the outermost set.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 2:}

\medskip
\noindent
Evaluate the expression: $\dfrac{(5 - 2)^3 + 3}{10 - 2^2}$\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Simplify the Numerator: \\
- Inside parenthesis: $5 - 2 = 3$. \\
- Apply exponent: $3^3 = 27$. \\
- Add: $27 + 3 = 30$. \\
2) Simplify the Denominator: \\
- Apply exponent: $2^2 = 4$. \\
- Subtract: $10 - 4 = 6$. \\
3) Divide Numerator by Denominator: $\dfrac{30}{6} = 5$.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}
