\noindent
Write and evaluate exponential expressions for each scenario.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A cube has a side length of 4 inches. Express its volume in exponential form, then evaluate it.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A population of bacteria doubles every hour. If there are 2 bacteria initially, write an expression using exponents for the number of bacteria after 4 hours, then evaluate it.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item You have a square painting with a side length of 10 inches. Express its area in exponential form, then evaluate it.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\end{enumerate}
