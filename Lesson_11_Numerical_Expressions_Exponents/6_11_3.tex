\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Word Problem Expressions}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Write a single numerical expression for each situation and evaluate it.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A book club buys 5 hardback books for \$12 each and 4 paperbacks for \$8 each. They receive a discount of \$5. What is the total cost?

\vspace{2cm}

\item A square garden has sides of length 6 feet. Inside, there is a square flowerbed with sides of 2 feet. Write an expression for the remaining area of the garden, and evaluate it.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A teacher orders 3 boxes of markers, each containing 12 markers, and 4 packs of colored pencils, each containing 8 pencils. Write a numerical expression for the total number of writing utensils, and evaluate it.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}
