\noindent
Show your detailed planning work for all parts of the question.

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item The student council is planning a school dance. They expect 150 students to attend. \\
\textbf{a)} The ratio of pizzas to students is planned to be 1:6. How many pizzas should they order? \\
\blanklong \\
\textbf{b)} Each pizza costs \$12.50. The pizza parlor offers a $12\%$ discount on orders of 20 or more pizzas. What is the cost of the pizzas before tax? \\
\blanklong \\
\textbf{c)} The sales tax rate is $8\%$. Find the total cost of the pizzas including tax. \\
\blanklong \\
\textbf{d)} If the student council has a total budget of \$500 for the dance, how much budget remains for decorations and music after buying the pizzas? \\
\blanklong
\end{enumerate}
