\vspace{3mm}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item The student council is planning a school dance. They expect 150 students to attend. \\
\textbf{a)} The ratio of pizzas to students is planned to be 1:6. How many pizzas should they order? \blanklong \\
\textbf{b)} Each pizza costs \$12.50. The pizza parlor offers a $12\%$ discount on orders of 20 or more pizzas. What is the cost of the pizzas before tax? \blanklong \\
\textbf{c)} The sales tax rate is $8\%$. Find the total cost of the pizzas including tax. \blanklong \\
\textbf{d)} If the student council has a total budget of \$500 for the dance, how much budget remains for decorations and music after buying the pizzas? \blanklong
\vspace{1.5cm}
\item A student council member records the ticket sales for the dance over 5 days: 12, 18, 25, 20, and 35 tickets. \\
\textbf{a)} Calculate the mean and range of daily ticket sales. \blanklong \\
\textbf{b)} If they want to reach a total of 150 tickets sold by the 6th day, how many tickets must they sell on the 6th day? \blanklong
\vspace{1.5cm}
\end{enumerate}
