\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Cumulative Problem Solving Strategies (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Solving complex, multi-step real-world problems requires integrating skills across multiple domains: ratios, fractions, decimal arithmetic, equations, geometry, and statistics. \\
\textbf{Key Steps:} \\
1. \textbf{Understand:} Identify the given values and what you need to find. \\
2. \textbf{Plan:} Choose the formulas or representations (tables, equations, diagrams) to use. \\
3. \textbf{Execute:} Perform the mathematical operations carefully. \\
4. \textbf{Verify:} Check if the answer is reasonable in the context.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}

\medskip
\noindent
A rectangular picture frame measures $8\text{ in} \times 10\text{ in}$. If the frame itself is $1.5\text{ in}$ wide all around, what is the area of the picture that fits inside?\\
\textbf{Solution:}\\
1) The picture is smaller than the frame on all 4 sides. \\
2) New Length $= 10 - 2(1.5) = 10 - 3 = 7\text{ inches}$. \\
3) New Width $= 8 - 2(1.5) = 8 - 3 = 5\text{ inches}$. \\
4) Area $= 7 \times 5 = 35\text{ square inches}$.
\end{tcolorbox}
