\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Statistical Questions and Distribution Characteristics (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Statistical Question:} A question that anticipates variability in the data (i.e., there is not just one single answer). \\
- *Non-statistical:* ``How old am I?'' (only 1 answer) \\
- *Statistical:* ``How old are the students in my class?'' (answers will vary) \\
2. \textbf{Data Distribution:} Summarized by its: \\
- **Center:** The average or middle value (mean, median). \\
- **Spread:** How spread out the data is (range, interquartile range). \\
- **Shape:** Symmetric, skewed left (long tail on left), or skewed right (long tail on right).

\vspace{4mm}
