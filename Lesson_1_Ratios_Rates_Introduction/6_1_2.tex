\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Quick Review: Ratios}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
A \textbf{ratio} compares quantities. \textbf{Part-to-part} ratios compare two parts. \textbf{Part-to-whole} ratios compare a part to everything.

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
% PART A — INTERPRET AND CREATE RATIOS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Interpret Ratios}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Write what each ratio tells you. Use ``for every'' language.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1.3cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item The ratio of red cars to blue cars is 2:5.

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item The ratio of cats to all pets is 3:7.

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item The ratio of teachers to students is 1:20.

\vspace{1.2cm}

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART D — REAL-WORLD RATIO APPLICATIONS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Apply Ratios to Real Situations}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each problem.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A punch recipe has a ratio of juice to soda of 3:2. If you use 6 cups of juice, how much soda do you need? How much punch total?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item In a parking lot, the ratio of trucks to cars is 1:4. If there are 8 trucks, how many cars are there? How many vehicles total?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item A drawing is scaled so the ratio of length to width is 5:3. If the length is 15 inches, what is the width?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item A team has twice as many forwards as goalies. If there are 6 goalies, write the ratio of forwards to goalies. How many forwards are there?

\vspace{1.8cm}

\end{enumerate}
