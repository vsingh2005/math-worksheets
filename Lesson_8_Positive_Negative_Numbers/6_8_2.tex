% ==========================================================
% PART C — SOLVING SITUATIONS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Solving Situations}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Represent and solve each word problem.

\vspace{3mm}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.3cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item A hiker starts at 30 feet below sea level. They climb up 75 feet. What is their new elevation? (Write as an integer.)

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A stock's price starts at \$42.00. On Monday it changes by $-\$3.50$. On Tuesday it changes by $+\$1.20$. What is the stock's price at the end of Tuesday?

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A submarine is at $-250$ meters. It rises 80 meters. Then it dives another 45 meters. What is its final depth?

\vspace{2.2cm}

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART D — CHALLENGE PROBLEMS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Challenge Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Reason through these multi-step integer situations.

\vspace{3mm}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.4cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item The temperature in Fairbanks, Alaska was $-22^\circ\text{F}$ in the morning. By noon it had risen $18^\circ\text{F}$. By evening it had dropped $14^\circ\text{F}$. What was the evening temperature?

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A football team gains 8 yards on first down, loses 5 yards on second down, and gains 12 yards on third down. What is the team's net gain or loss over three plays?

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item Two numbers are opposites. The sum of their absolute values is 36. What are the two numbers? Explain your reasoning.

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}
