\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Area of Triangles and Quadrilaterals (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Parallelogram:} $Area = base \times height = b \times h$ \\
2. \textbf{Triangle:} $Area = \frac{1}{2} \times base \times height = \frac{1}{2}bh$ \\
- *Why?* A triangle is exactly half of a parallelogram with the same base and height. \\
3. \textbf{Trapezoid:} $Area = \frac{1}{2}(base_1 + base_2) \times height = \frac{1}{2}(a+b)h$

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}

\medskip
\noindent
Find the area of a triangle with a base of 6 cm and a height of 4 cm.\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
$$Area = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 4 = 12\text{ cm}^2.$$
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}
