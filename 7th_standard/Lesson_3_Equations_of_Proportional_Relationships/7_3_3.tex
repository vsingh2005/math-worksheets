\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Lesson 3: Equations of Proportional Relationships}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
A proportional relationship can be represented by the equation $y = kx$, where $k$ is the constant of proportionality (also known as the unit rate).

\begin{itemize}[label=$\bullet$, itemsep=2mm]
    \item The point $(0,0)$ on the graph of a proportional relationship always represents the starting point where there is zero of one quantity and zero of the other.
    \item The point $(1, k)$ on the graph represents the unit rate. It shows that for every 1 unit of $x$, there are $k$ units of $y$.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{5mm}
