\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Instruction: Understanding Scale Factors}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
A \textbf{scale drawing} is a proportional, two-dimensional drawing of an object. The scale tells you how the drawing's dimensions relate to the actual object's dimensions.

\vspace{2mm}
\noindent
\textbf{Setting up a Proportion:} \\
To find a missing dimension, set up a proportion comparing the \textbf{drawing length} to the \textbf{actual length}.
\[ \dfrac{\text{Scale Drawing Length}}{\text{Scale Actual Length}} = \dfrac{\text{Measured Drawing Length}}{\text{Actual Target Length}} \]

\vspace{2mm}
\noindent
\textbf{Example:} A map has a scale of $1\text{ inch} = 10\text{ miles}$. The distance between two cities on the map is $3.5\text{ inches}$. What is the actual distance?
\[ \dfrac{1\text{ in}}{10\text{ mi}} = \dfrac{3.5\text{ in}}{x\text{ mi}} \]
Cross multiply: $1 \times x = 10 \times 3.5 \implies x = 35\text{ miles}$.

\vspace{5mm}

\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Practice: Finding Actual Lengths}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Use the given scale to find the actual length or the drawing length. Show your proportion.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=4cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item \textbf{Scale:} $1\text{ cm} = 5\text{ m}$ \\
The length of a park on a map is $12\text{ cm}$. What is the actual length of the park?

\item \textbf{Scale:} $2\text{ inches} = 15\text{ feet}$ \\
The actual height of a building is $90\text{ feet}$. How tall should the building be drawn on the blueprint?

\item \textbf{Scale:} $\dfrac{1}{2}\text{ inch} = 3\text{ miles}$ \\
On a map, the distance from your house to the beach is $4\dfrac{1}{2}\text{ inches}$. What is the actual distance?

\item \textbf{Scale:} $1\text{ cm} = 50\text{ km}$ \\
The actual distance between two cities is $325\text{ km}$. How far apart are they on the map?

\end{enumerate}
