% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part A: Finding Missing Dimensions from Area}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Work backwards to find the missing dimensions.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A blueprint uses a scale of $1\text{ inch} = 8\text{ feet}$. The actual area of a rectangular bedroom is $192\text{ square feet}$. If the actual length is $16\text{ feet}$, what is the width of the bedroom on the blueprint?

\item A map has a scale of $2\text{ cm} = 15\text{ km}$. A rectangular forest has an actual area of $900\text{ square km}$. If the length of the forest on the map is $8\text{ cm}$, what is the actual width of the forest?

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART B - CHALLENGE PROBLEMS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: Real-World Scale Challenges}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each multi-step scale problem. Show all of your steps clearly.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item A statue is $45\text{ feet}$ tall. A model of the statue is created using a scale where $\dfrac{1}{4}\text{ inch} = 1.5\text{ feet}$. The creator of the model wants to put it in a display case that is $8\text{ inches}$ tall. Will the model fit inside the display case? Justify your answer with math.

\item You are painting a large mural on a wall that is $20\text{ feet}$ long and $12\text{ feet}$ high. You make a sketch of the mural on a piece of paper that is $10\text{ inches}$ long and $6\text{ inches}$ high. What is the scale factor of the drawing to the actual mural? (Write your answer as a ratio of inches to feet).

\item An original photograph is $4\text{ inches}$ wide and $6\text{ inches}$ long. You want to enlarge it so the new length is $15\text{ inches}$. If you keep the picture proportional, what will the new area of the photograph be?

\end{enumerate}
