\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Instruction: Triangle Inequality Theorem}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
You cannot just take any three lengths and make a triangle. They must follow the \textbf{Triangle Inequality Theorem}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Rule:} The sum of the two shorter sides of a triangle MUST be \textit{strictly greater than} the longest side.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2mm}
\noindent
\textbf{Example:} Can you make a triangle with sides $3\text{ cm}$, $4\text{ cm}$, and $8\text{ cm}$?
\begin{itemize}
    \item Shorter sides: $3 + 4 = 7$
    \item Is $7 > 8$? \textbf{No!}
    \item So, these sides \textbf{cannot} form a triangle. They won't reach each other to close the shape!
\end{itemize}

\vspace{5mm}
