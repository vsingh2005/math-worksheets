\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part C: Real-World Inequalities}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Write an inequality to model each word problem, solve it, and interpret what the solution means.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item You want to buy a new bicycle that costs $\$250$. You already have $\$115$ saved. You earn $\$15$ per hour babysitting. Write and solve an inequality to find the minimum number of full hours, $h$, you must babysit to afford the bicycle.

\item A moving truck company charges a base fee of $\$35$ plus $\$0.50$ per mile. You have a maximum budget of $\$100$ for moving expenses. Write and solve an inequality to find the maximum number of miles, $m$, you can drive the truck without going over budget.

\item The school's elevator has a maximum weight capacity of $2,000$ pounds. If the elevator car itself weighs $650$ pounds, and each person getting on the elevator weighs an average of $150$ pounds, write and solve an inequality to determine the maximum number of people, $p$, that can ride the elevator at once.

\end{enumerate}
