\item A club is having a car wash to raise money. They need to raise at least $\$300$. They have already received $\$45$ in donations. They charge $\$8$ per car wash. Write and solve an inequality to find the minimum number of cars they must wash to reach their goal.

\item Your cell phone plan gives you $5$ gigabytes of data for $\$40$ a month, plus $\$10$ for each additional gigabyte of data you use. \item You have a $\$50$ gift card to an online music store. You buy a premium membership for $\$15$ and then want to download songs that cost $\$1.25$ each. What is the greatest number of songs you can download without exceeding the gift card balance?

\end{enumerate}
