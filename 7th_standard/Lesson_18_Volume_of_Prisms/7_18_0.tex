\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Instruction: The Universal Volume Formula}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
To find the volume of \textit{any} right prism (rectangular, triangular, hexagonal, etc.), you only need one master formula:
\[ \mathbf{V = B \times h} \]
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{V:} Volume (how much space is inside, measured in cubic units like $\text{cm}^3$)
    \item \textbf{B:} Area of the BASE shape (Notice it is a capital 'B')
    \item \textbf{h:} Height of the prism (the distance between the two parallel bases)
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2mm}
\noindent
\textbf{Steps to solve:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Identify the base shape (e.g., triangle, rectangle).
    \item Find the area of that 2D base shape ($B$).
    \item Multiply that area by the height of the 3D prism ($h$).
\end{enumerate}
