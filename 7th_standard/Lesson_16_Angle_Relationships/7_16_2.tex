\item Three adjacent angles lie on a straight line, so their sum is $180^\circ$. They measure $45^\circ$, $x^\circ$, and $(2x)^\circ$. Write an equation, solve for $x$, and find the measure of the unknown angles.

\item Two angles are complementary. Angle $A$ is four times larger than Angle $B$. Let Angle $B = x$. Write an equation to find the measure of both angles.

\end{enumerate}
