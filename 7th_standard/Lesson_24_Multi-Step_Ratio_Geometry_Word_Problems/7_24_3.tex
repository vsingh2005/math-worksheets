\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
This problem requires surface area, unit conversions, and percentage markups!

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A wooden toy block is shaped like a perfect cube with a side length of $4\text{ inches}$. A toy maker needs to paint $100$ of these blocks completely (all six sides of every block). 

One small can of paint covers exactly $5\text{ square feet}$ of surface area. (Note: $1\text{ square foot} = 144\text{ square inches}$).

A can of paint costs $\$8.00$ at the hardware store. However, the store is currently running a promotion where you pay a $10\%$ markup on the first can, but get a $50\%$ discount on all additional cans.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1:} What is the total surface area (in square inches) that needs to be painted for all 100 blocks?
    \item \textbf{Step 2:} Convert that total area into square feet.
    \item \textbf{Step 3:} How many cans of paint must the toy maker buy? (You can't buy a fraction of a can).
    \item \textbf{Step 4:} What is the total final cost for the paint?
\end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}
