\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item A neighborhood is building a new community garden. The garden will be shaped like a large rectangle with a semicircle attached to one end. The rectangular part is $40\text{ feet}$ long and $20\text{ feet}$ wide. The semicircle is attached to the $20$-foot side (so the diameter of the semicircle is $20\text{ feet}$).

The neighborhood association needs to put a fence around the \textit{entire outside perimeter} of the garden to keep deer out. Fencing costs $\$12.50$ per foot. They also have a coupon for $15\%$ off their total fencing purchase. 

What is the final cost they will pay for the fence?

\textit{(Hint: You need to calculate the perimeter of three sides of the rectangle, plus the circumference of the semicircle. Then find the cost, then apply the discount.)}
\end{enumerate}
