\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
In this Capstone unit, you will combine multiple math skills to solve realistic, complex problems. A single problem might require you to:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Use a scale factor to find real-world dimensions.
    \item Use those dimensions to calculate an area or volume.
    \item Use a unit rate (cost per square foot, for example) to calculate a final price.
    \item Apply a percentage discount or tax to get the absolute final answer.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{2mm}
\noindent
\textbf{Strategy for Multi-Step Problems:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1: Understand.} What are they asking for in the end?
    \item \textbf{Step 2: Plan.} What mini-calculations do I need to do first? List them out.
    \item \textbf{Step 3: Execute.} Do the math step-by-step. Label your numbers!
    \item \textbf{Step 4: Check.} Does the final answer make logical sense?
\end{itemize}

Follow the steps to solve the compound problem.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=4.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A blueprint of a rectangular living room has a scale of $1\text{ inch} = 3\text{ feet}$. On the blueprint, the room measures $5\text{ inches}$ by $6\text{ inches}$. You want to buy new carpet, which costs $\$4.00$ per square foot. There is an $8\%$ sales tax. What is the total cost of the carpet?
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1:} Find the real-life dimensions of the room using the scale. \blanklong
    \item \textbf{Step 2:} Find the real-life Area of the room in square feet. \blanklong
    \item \textbf{Step 3:} Find the base cost of the carpet (Area $\times$ Unit Rate). \blanklong
    \item \textbf{Step 4:} Calculate the $8\%$ tax and add it to the base cost to find the total. \blanklong
\end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}
