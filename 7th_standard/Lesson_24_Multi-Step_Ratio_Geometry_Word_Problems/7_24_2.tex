\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A rectangular swimming pool is $25\text{ meters}$ long, $10\text{ meters}$ wide, and has a uniform depth of $2\text{ meters}$.

The city water department charges $\$1.50$ for every cubic meter of water. A water truck can pump water into the pool at a rate of $50\text{ cubic meters}$ per hour.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Question 1:} How much will it cost to completely fill the pool?
    \item \textbf{Question 2:} How many hours will it take to fill the pool?
\end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}
