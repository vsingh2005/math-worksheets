\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part C: Application Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each word problem by writing and simplifying an expression.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item You have a coupon for $25\%$ off the price of any item. If the original price of the item is $x$ dollars, write an expression for the final price after the discount. Write the expression in two different ways.

\item The perimeter of a triangle is $(14x + 6)$ inches. If two of the sides are $(3x - 1)$ inches and $(5x + 4)$ inches, write a simplified expression for the length of the third side.

\item A rectangular garden has a length of $(4x - 2)$ meters and a width of $5$ meters. A path is built around the garden, extending the total length by $2$ meters and the width by $2$ meters. Write a simplified expression for the new total area.

\end{enumerate}
