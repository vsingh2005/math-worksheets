\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Instruction: Expanding \& Factoring Expressions}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
\textbf{1. Distributive Property (Expanding):} Multiply the outside term by every term inside the parentheses.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Example: $\dfrac{1}{2}(4x - 6) \implies \left(\dfrac{1}{2} \times 4x\right) - \left(\dfrac{1}{2} \times 6\right) \implies 2x - 3$
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2mm}

\noindent
\textbf{2. Factoring Expressions:} Find the greatest common factor (GCF) of all terms, and "reverse distribute" it.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Example: $15x + 25 \implies$ The GCF of $15$ and $25$ is $5$. $\implies 5(3x + 5)$
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2mm}

\noindent
\textbf{3. Combining Like Terms:} Add or subtract the coefficients of terms that have the exact same variable.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Example: $2.5y + 1.5y - 0.5y = (2.5 + 1.5 - 0.5)y = 3.5y$
\end{itemize}

\vspace{5mm}
