\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item The price of a skateboard is $p$ dollars. The store marks up the price by $15\%$. Write two different but equivalent expressions for the final selling price of the skateboard.

\item The length of a rectangle is $(3x + 5)$ and its width is $(2x - 1)$. Write a simplified expression for the perimeter of the rectangle. 

\item A plumber charges a flat fee of $\$50$ just to show up, plus $\$65$ per hour of work. If the plumber works $h$ hours on Monday and $(h + 2)$ hours on Tuesday, write a simplified expression for the total amount charged across both days.

\end{enumerate}
