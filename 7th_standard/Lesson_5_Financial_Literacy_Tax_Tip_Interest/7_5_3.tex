% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part A: Sales Tax, Tips, and Commissions}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Solve each problem. Show all of your percent equations or proportions, and round your final answers to the nearest cent where appropriate.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A family's dinner bill at a restaurant is $\$65.00$. They want to leave an $18\%$ tip on the pre-tax bill, and the sales tax rate is $8\%$. What is the total cost of the dinner including tax and tip?

\item Marcus is a real estate agent. He earns a $3\%$ commission on the selling price of every house he sells. If he sells a house for $\$285,000$, how much commission does Marcus earn?

\item A winter jacket is priced at $\$80.00$. After sales tax is added, the total cost of the jacket is $\$86.80$. What is the sales tax rate as a percentage?

\item An electronics store sells a tablet for $\$350$. If the sales tax is $6\%$ and the customer also buys a protection plan that costs $10\%$ of the tablet's original price, what is the total cost of the purchase?

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART B — MARKUPS AND MARKDOWNS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: Markups and Markdowns}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each retail-themed problem. Show your steps clearly.

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A boutique owner buys designer shoes at a wholesale price of $\$45.00$ per pair. She marks up the price by $120\%$ to sell them in her store. What is the retail price of the shoes?

\item A bicycle originally priced at $\$420$ is on sale for $25\%$ off. If there is a $5\%$ sales tax calculated on the discounted sale price, what is the final cost of the bicycle?

\item A comfortable living room couch is on sale for $30\%$ off its original price. If the sale price is $\$560$, what was the original price of the couch before the markdown?

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART C — SIMPLE INTEREST
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part C: Simple Interest ($I = Prt$)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Use the simple interest formula $I = Prt$ (where $I$ is interest, $P$ is principal, $r$ is annual interest rate as a decimal, and $t$ is time in years) to solve each problem.

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item Sarah deposits $\$1,500$ into a savings account that earns $4\%$ simple interest per year. How much interest will she earn after $5$ years?

\item James borrows $\$8,000$ from a bank to buy a used car. The loan has a simple interest rate of $6.5\%$ per year. How much total money (principal plus interest) will he pay back to the bank after $3$ years?

\item Elena invests $\$2,400$ in a certificate of deposit (CD) that pays $3\%$ annual simple interest. How much interest will she have earned at the end of $18$ months? (\textit{Hint: Convert months to years first!})

\end{enumerate}
