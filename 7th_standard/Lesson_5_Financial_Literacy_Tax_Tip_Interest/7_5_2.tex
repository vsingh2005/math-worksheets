% PART A — TAX, TIP, & MARKUP
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part A: Tax, Tip, and Markups}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Solve each problem. Show your setup, including any decimal multipliers or proportions used. Round your final answers to the nearest cent where necessary.

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A family went out to dinner and their meal cost $\$64.00$. They want to leave an $18\%$ tip for the server. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item How much money should they leave for the tip?
    \item What is the total cost of the meal including the tip?
\end{itemize}

\item A sporting goods store buys skateboards from a manufacturer for $\$45$ each. The store marks up the price by $60\%$ before selling them to the public. What is the retail price of a skateboard?

\item You purchase a new winter coat for $\$85.00$. If the sales tax rate in your city is $7.5\%$, what is the total amount you will pay at the register?

\item A salon charges $\$48.00$ for a haircut. The customer wants to give the stylist a $20\%$ tip. If there is also an $8\%$ sales tax applied to the service fee (before tip), what is the total amount paid?

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART B — DISCOUNTS, MARKDOWNS, & COMMISSION
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: Discounts, Markdowns, and Commission}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each problem. Show all intermediate steps clearly.

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A laptop regularly costs $\$750.00$. It is currently on sale for $15\%$ off. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is the discount amount?
    \item What is the sale price of the laptop before tax?
\end{itemize}

\item A real estate agent earns a $2.5\%$ commission on the selling price of any home they sell. If they sell a house for $\$340,000$, how much commission does the agent earn?

\item A department store marks down a designer handbag by $30\%$. The original price of the handbag was $\$120.00$. If a customer has a coupon for an \textit{additional} $10\%$ off the sale price, what is the final price of the handbag? 

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART C — SIMPLE INTEREST
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part C: Simple Interest ($I = Prt$)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Use the simple interest formula $I = Prt$ to solve the following problems. Identify the values of $P$, $r$, and $t$ before calculating.

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item You deposit $\$1,200$ into a savings account that earns $3\%$ simple annual interest. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item How much interest will you earn after $5$ years?
    \item What will be the total balance in the account after $5$ years?
\end{itemize}

\item Marcus borrowed $\$4,500$ from the bank at a simple interest rate of $6\%$ per year to buy a used car. If it takes him $18$ months to pay back the loan, how much total interest will he owe? (\textit{Hint: Convert months to years first!})

\item An investment of $\$2,500$ earned $\$375$ in simple interest over a period of $3$ years. What was the annual simple interest rate of the investment?

\end{enumerate}
