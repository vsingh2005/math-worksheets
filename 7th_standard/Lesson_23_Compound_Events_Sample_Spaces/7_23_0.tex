\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Instruction: What is a Compound Event?}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
A \textbf{Simple Event} is one action (like rolling a single die). \\
A \textbf{Compound Event} consists of two or more simple events happening together (like rolling a die AND flipping a coin).

\vspace{2mm}
\noindent
\textbf{The Sample Space:}
The sample space is the list of ALL possible outcomes for a compound event. You can organize the sample space using:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Organized Lists:} e.g., (Heads, 1), (Heads, 2), etc.
    \item \textbf{Tables:} Great for exactly two events (e.g., rows for coin flips, columns for die rolls).
    \item \textbf{Tree Diagrams:} Great for mapping out sequence of choices.
\end{itemize}
