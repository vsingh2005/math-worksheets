\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Find the total area of the composite figure. Show your calculations for each part before adding them together.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A composite figure is made of a rectangle measuring $8\text{ cm}$ by $5\text{ cm}$, and a triangle attached to the $8\text{ cm}$ side with a height of $4\text{ cm}$.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Area of Rectangle: \blanklong
    \item Area of Triangle: \blanklong
    \item Total Area: \blanklong
\end{itemize}

\item An ice cream cone shape is drawn on paper. The triangle part has a base of $6\text{ inches}$ and a height of $10\text{ inches}$. A semicircle is attached to the $6$-inch base. Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Area of Triangle: \blanklong
    \item Area of Semicircle: \blanklong
    \item Total Area: \blanklong
\end{itemize}

\item An L-shaped garden can be split into two rectangles. Rectangle 1 is $10\text{ ft}$ by $4\text{ ft}$. Rectangle 2 is $6\text{ ft}$ by $5\text{ ft}$. What is the total area of the garden?

\end{enumerate}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: Holes and Borders}}

\noindent
Find the area of the shaded region by subtracting the inner shape from the outer shape.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item A rectangular picture frame has outer dimensions of $12\text{ in}$ by $15\text{ in}$. The hole for the picture inside is $8\text{ in}$ by $10\text{ in}$. What is the area of just the frame?

\item A circular dartboard has a radius of $10\text{ cm}$. The bullseye in the center has a radius of $2\text{ cm}$. What is the area of the dartboard \textit{excluding} the bullseye?

\end{enumerate}
