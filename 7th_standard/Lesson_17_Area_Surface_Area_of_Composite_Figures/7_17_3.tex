% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part A: Real-World Costs}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Calculate the area and then find the total cost of materials.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A family is building a wooden deck in the shape of a rectangle with a triangle attached. The rectangle is $15\text{ feet}$ by $20\text{ feet}$. The triangle has a base of $15\text{ feet}$ and a height of $8\text{ feet}$. The decking wood costs $\$4.50$ per square foot. What is the total cost of the wood for the deck?

\item A community center wants to paint the four walls of a rectangular gymnasium. The gym is $40\text{ feet}$ long, $30\text{ feet}$ wide, and $15\text{ feet}$ high. There are two doors, each $7\text{ feet}$ tall and $3\text{ feet}$ wide, and four windows, each $4\text{ feet}$ by $5\text{ feet}$, which will not be painted. If one gallon of paint covers $400\text{ square feet}$, how many gallons of paint do they need to buy?

\end{enumerate}

\vspace{5cm}

% ==========================================================
% PART B - CHALLENGE PROBLEM
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: The Doughnut Problem}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Use your problem-solving skills and your knowledge of circles. Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item A circular plaza has a radius of $30\text{ meters}$. In the exact center of the plaza is a circular fountain with a diameter of $10\text{ meters}$. The city wants to pave the area of the plaza \textit{around} the fountain with cobblestones.
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is the area of the entire plaza (including the fountain space)?
    \item What is the area of the fountain?
    \item How many square meters of cobblestone will the city need to buy?
\end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}
