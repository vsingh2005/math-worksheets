\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Instruction: Order of Operations with Rational Numbers}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

When evaluating expressions that include addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division with rational numbers (decimals, fractions, and integers), you must strictly follow the \textbf{Order of Operations (PEMDAS)}:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{P}arentheses (and other grouping symbols like brackets or fraction bars)
    \item \textbf{E}xponents
    \item \textbf{M}ultiply and \textbf{D}ivide (from left to right)
    \item \textbf{A}dd and \textbf{S}ubtract (from left to right)
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Example:} Evaluate $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) + \dfrac{3}{4} \times \left(-2\right)$

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1 (Multiply):} $\dfrac{3}{4} \times \left(-\dfrac{2}{1}\right) = -\dfrac{6}{4} = -1\dfrac{1}{2}$
    \item \textbf{Step 2 (Add):} $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) + \left(-1\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -2$
\end{itemize}
