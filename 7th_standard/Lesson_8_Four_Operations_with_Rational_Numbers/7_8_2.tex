\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=\fill, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item You are playing a board game. On your first turn, you lose $15$ points. On your second turn, you gain $8$ points. On your third turn, your total score is multiplied by $-2$. What is your final score?

\item A rectangular pool is $12.5$ feet long and $8.4$ feet wide. If a path that is $2$ feet wide is built around the entire pool, what is the new total area of the pool and the path?

\item In a science experiment, the temperature of a chemical starts at $20^\circ$C. It decreases by $4.5^\circ$C every minute for $8$ minutes. What is the final temperature of the chemical?

\item You buy $4$ books that cost $\$12.50$ each and $2$ posters that cost $\$6.75$ each. You have a coupon for $\$10$ off your total purchase. If you pay with a $\$100$ bill, how much change will you receive?

\end{enumerate}
