\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Instruction: Uniform and Non-Uniform Models}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
A \textbf{Probability Model} is just a list of all the possible outcomes of an event and the probability of each outcome occurring. The sum of all probabilities in a model MUST exactly equal $1$.

\vspace{2mm}
\noindent
\textbf{Types of Models:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Uniform Probability Model:} Every single outcome has the exact same chance of happening. (e.g., flipping a fair coin or rolling a standard die).
    \item \textbf{Non-Uniform Probability Model:} Some outcomes are more likely to happen than others. (e.g., a spinner where the "Red" section takes up half the circle, but "Blue" only takes up a quarter).
\end{itemize}
