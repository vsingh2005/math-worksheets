% PART A — COMPUTING UNIT RATES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part A: Find the Unit Rate}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Compute the unit rate for each problem. Be sure to simplify your fraction if possible!

\vspace{1.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A baker frosts $\dfrac{4}{5}$ of a cake in $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of an hour. How many cakes can they frost per hour?

\item A hiker walks $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of a mile in $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of an hour. What is their speed in miles per hour?

\item A water tank leaks $\dfrac{2}{7}$ of a gallon in $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a minute. How many gallons does it leak per minute?

\item A student reads $\dfrac{3}{10}$ of a book in $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of an hour. How many books do they read per hour?

\item A landscaper mows $\dfrac{7}{8}$ of an acre in $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of an hour. How many acres do they mow per hour?

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART B — COMPARING UNIT RATES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: Comparing Rates and Best Value}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Find the unit rate for both options, and then state which is the better deal or faster rate.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item \textbf{Gas Station A} sells $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a tank of gas for $\$12$. \\
\textbf{Gas Station B} sells $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a tank of gas for $\$15$. \\
Which station is cheaper per full tank?

\item \textbf{Swimmer A} swims $\dfrac{4}{5}$ of a lap in $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a minute. \\
\textbf{Swimmer B} swims $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of a lap in $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a minute. \\
Who is the faster swimmer?

\item \textbf{Farmer 1} harvests $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of a field in $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of a day. \\
\textbf{Farmer 2} harvests $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a field in $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of a day. \\
Who harvests more of the field per day?

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART C — WORD PROBLEMS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part C: Real-World Applications}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each problem by finding the unit rate first. Show all of your work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A chef uses $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of a pound of cheese for every $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a pizza.
\begin{itemize}
    \item How much cheese is used per full pizza?
    \item How much cheese is needed to make $6$ pizzas?
\end{itemize}

\item A car uses $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a gallon of gas to travel $\dfrac{1}{8}$ of the distance between two cities.
\begin{itemize}
    \item How many gallons are needed for the full distance?
    \item If gas costs $\$4$ per gallon, how much will the full trip cost?
\end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}
