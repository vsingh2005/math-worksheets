% PART A — COMPUTING UNIT RATES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part A: Find the Unit Rate}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
Compute the unit rate for each problem. Show the complex fraction and the keep-change-flip process.

\vspace{1.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A runner jogs $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of a mile in $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of an hour. What is their speed in miles per hour?

\item A cookie recipe requires $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a cup of flour to make $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a batch. How much flour is needed for a full batch?

\item A machine paints $\dfrac{4}{5}$ of a square foot in $\dfrac{1}{10}$ of a minute. How many square feet does it paint per minute?

\item You hike $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of a mile in $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of an hour. What is your speed in miles per hour?

\item A hose fills $\dfrac{7}{8}$ of a pool in $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of an hour. How much of the pool is filled per hour?

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART B — COMPARING UNIT RATES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: Comparing Rates and Best Value}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Find the unit rate for both options, and then state which is the better deal or faster rate.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item \textbf{Car A} travels $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a mile in $\dfrac{1}{60}$ of an hour. \\
\textbf{Car B} travels $\dfrac{4}{5}$ of a mile in $\dfrac{1}{50}$ of an hour. \\
Which car is faster? (Find miles per hour for both)

\item \textbf{Store A} sells $\dfrac{1}{2}$ pound of almonds for $\$5$. \\
\textbf{Store B} sells $\dfrac{3}{4}$ pound of almonds for $\$7$. \\
Which store has the cheaper price per pound?

\item \textbf{Recipe 1} uses $\dfrac{2}{3}$ cup of sugar for $\dfrac{1}{2}$ batch of muffins. \\
\textbf{Recipe 2} uses $\dfrac{3}{4}$ cup of sugar for $\dfrac{2}{3}$ batch of muffins. \\
Which recipe uses more sugar per full batch?

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART C — WORD PROBLEMS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part C: Multi-Step Word Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each problem by finding the unit rate first.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A cyclist rides $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of a mile in $\dfrac{1}{10}$ of an hour. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is the cyclist's speed in miles per hour?
    \item At this rate, how far will the cyclist ride in $2.5$ hours?
\end{itemize}

\item A pump can empty $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of a tank in $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of an hour.
\begin{itemize}
    \item How much of the tank does it empty in $1$ hour?
    \item How long will it take to empty $5$ full tanks?
\end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}
