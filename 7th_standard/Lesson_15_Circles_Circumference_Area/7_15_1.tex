\noindent
Use the given information to find the missing dimensions. Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item The circumference of a circle is $31.4\text{ cm}$. What is the diameter?

\item The circumference of a circle is $50.24\text{ inches}$. What is the radius?

\item The area of a circle is $78.5\text{ square meters}$. What is the radius? (Hint: Divide by $\pi$ first, then think about what number squared equals your result).

\end{enumerate}

\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: Real-World Applications}}

\noindent
Solve each word problem. Make sure to identify whether the problem is asking for Circumference or Area.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item A circular dining table has a diameter of $6\text{ feet}$. You want to buy a tablecloth that perfectly covers the top of the table. How many square feet of fabric is needed?

\item A dog is tied to a post in the backyard with a leash that is $10\text{ feet}$ long. What is the total area of the grass that the dog can reach?

\item A Ferris wheel has a radius of $40\text{ feet}$. How far will you travel in one complete rotation on the Ferris wheel?

\end{enumerate}
