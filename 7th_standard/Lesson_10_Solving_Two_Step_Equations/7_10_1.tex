\noindent
Solve equations of the form $p(x + q) = r$. You can either distribute first, or divide both sides by $p$ first. Show your work.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item $3(x + 4) = 24$

\item $-2(y - 5) = 14$

\item $\dfrac{1}{2}(m + 8) = 10$

\item $5(2x - 1) = 35$

\end{enumerate}
\end{multicols}

% ==========================================================
% PART C - TRANSLATING TO EQUATIONS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part C: Real-World Applications}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
For each word problem, write an equation and solve it.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item You buy a notebook for $\$3$ and $4$ identical pens. Your total cost is $\$15$. Write an equation to find the cost of each pen, $p$, and solve it.

\item A rectangular garden has a perimeter of $40$ feet. The width is $8$ feet. Let $L$ represent the length of the garden. The formula for perimeter is $2(L + W) = P$. Write the equation and solve for $L$.

\item A plumber charges a flat fee of $\$40$ plus $\$60$ per hour of work. If the total bill was $\$220$, write and solve an equation to find $h$, the number of hours the plumber worked.

\end{enumerate}
