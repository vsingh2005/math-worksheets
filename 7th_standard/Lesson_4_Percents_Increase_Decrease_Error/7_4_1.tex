\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item A department store buys a pair of shoes for \$45 and marks up the price by 40\%. What is the retail price of the shoes?
\item The population of a small town decreased from 12,500 residents in 2020 to 11,800 residents in 2023. What is the percent decrease in the population?
\item A tech company reduced the weight of its flagship laptop by 15\%. The new model weighs 3.4 pounds. What was the weight of the original laptop?
\end{enumerate}

% --- PART B ---
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Part B: Percent Increase and Decrease}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]
\item Last year, Sarah earned \$18.00 per hour at her job. This year, she received a raise and now earns \$20.70 per hour. What was the percent increase in her hourly wage?
\item A furniture store purchases a couch for \$350. They mark up the price by 80\% to sell it retail. During a holiday clearance sale, they offer a 25\% discount on the retail price. What is the final sale price of the couch?
\item Marcus is a car salesman who earns a base salary of \$2,000 per month plus a 4\% commission on his total sales. Last month, his total sales were \$48,500. However, a \$120 processing fee is deducted from his monthly earnings. What was Marcus's net take-home pay last month?
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]
\item The subtotal of a family's dinner at a restaurant is \$85.00. 
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=\fill]
\item Calculate the total cost including an 8\% sales tax.
\item Calculate the final total if they also leave a 15\% tip based on the original subtotal (before tax).
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]
\item Maya estimates that the length of her classroom whiteboard is 3.5 meters. The actual length of the whiteboard is 3.8 meters. What is the percent error of Maya's estimate?
\item A chemistry student measures the mass of a copper sample to be 42.4 grams. The actual mass of the copper sample is 40.0 grams. Find the percent error in the student's measurement.
\item An event organizer estimated that 250 people would attend a charity gala. The actual attendance was 215 people. What was the percent error of the organizer's estimate?
\end{enumerate}
