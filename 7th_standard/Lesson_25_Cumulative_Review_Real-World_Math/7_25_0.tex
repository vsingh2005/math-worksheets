\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Practice: The Business Owner}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
You are opening a small bakery. Answer the questions to get your business off the ground.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=4.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item \textbf{Recipe Ratios:} Your master recipe for chocolate chip cookies requires $2\frac{1}{2}$ cups of flour to make $30$ cookies. You received a massive catering order for $450$ cookies. How many cups of flour do you need to buy? \textit{(Show your proportion).}

\item \textbf{Financial Equations:} Your monthly rent is $\$1,200$. Ingredients cost $\$0.50$ per cookie. You sell each cookie for $\$2.50$. Write and solve an inequality to find the minimum number of cookies ($c$) you must sell to make a \textit{profit} of at least $\$800$ this month. \textit{(Profit = Total Sales - Total Costs).}

\end{enumerate}
