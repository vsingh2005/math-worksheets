\noindent
You work for a tech company that is testing a new app.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item \textbf{Statistics \& Sampling:} You send a survey to a random sample of $400$ users out of your total population of $25,000$ users. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item $180$ users say the app is "Excellent".
    \item $150$ users say the app is "Okay".
    \item $70$ users say the app is "Terrible" because it crashes.
\end{itemize}

Based on this sample, predict exactly how many of the $25,000$ total users think the app is "Terrible." 

If it costs the company $\$15$ in tech support time for every user who thinks the app is "Terrible," how much money should the company budget for tech support this month? Write out the full equation.

\end{enumerate}
