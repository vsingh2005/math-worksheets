\noindent
You are a city planner in charge of renovating a local park.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item \textbf{Scale \& Area:} The blueprint of the park is a rectangle measuring $8\text{ inches}$ by $12\text{ inches}$. The scale on the blueprint is $1\text{ inch} = 15\text{ yards}$. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is the actual area of the park in square yards? 
    \item The city is laying down new grass. One bag of grass seed covers $400\text{ square yards}$ and costs $\$45.50$. How much will it cost to seed the entire park?
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Probability \& Geometry:} A circular fountain with a radius of $5\text{ yards}$ is going to be built perfectly in the center of the park. If a skydiver jumps out of an airplane and lands perfectly at a random spot inside the park, what is the probability that they land IN the water fountain? (Express your answer as a percentage rounded to the nearest tenth).

\end{enumerate}
