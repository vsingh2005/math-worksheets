\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A rectangular block of cheese measures $6\text{ inches}$ by $4\text{ inches}$ on its base and is $10\text{ inches}$ tall. You use a wire to slice the cheese parallel to the base. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is the shape of the slice? \blank
    \item What are the dimensions of the slice? \blank
    \item What is the area of the slice? \blank
\end{itemize}

\item You take the same block of cheese ($6\text{ in}$ by $4\text{ in}$ base, $10\text{ in}$ tall) and slice it perpendicular to the base, cutting right through the middle of the $6\text{ inch}$ side.
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is the shape of the slice? \blank
    \item What are the dimensions of the slice? \blank
\end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}

\noindent
Answer the questions about slicing a square pyramid.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3.0cm, leftmargin=0.6cm, resume]

\item A square pyramid has a base with side lengths of $5\text{ cm}$. You slice the pyramid parallel to the base, halfway up to the top. What shape is the cross section? Is the side length of this cross section equal to, less than, or greater than $5\text{ cm}$? Explain.

\item You slice the same square pyramid perpendicular to the base, cutting exactly through the top point (the apex) down to the middle of the base. What is the shape of the cross section?

\end{enumerate}
