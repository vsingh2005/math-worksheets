\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Algebraic Properties and Equivalency (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Properties:} \\
- **Commutative Property:** $a + b = b + a$, $ab = ba$ \\
- **Associative Property:** $(a+b)+c = a+(b+c)$, $(ab)c = a(bc)$ \\
- **Distributive Property:** $a(b+c) = ab + ac$, and $ab + ac = a(b+c)$ \\
2. \textbf{Combining Like Terms:} Add or subtract coefficients of terms with the same variable. Example: $3x + 5 + 2x - 1 = 5x + 4$.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}

\medskip
\noindent
Expand the expression $4(2x + 3)$ using the Distributive Property, then check equivalence by substituting $x=2$.\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Expand: $4 \times 2x + 4 \times 3 = 8x + 12$. \\
2) Check for $x=2$: \\
- Original: $4(2(2) + 3) = 4(4 + 3) = 4(7) = 28$. \\
- Expanded: $8(2) + 12 = 16 + 12 = 28$. \\
- Since both equal 28, the expressions are equivalent.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}
