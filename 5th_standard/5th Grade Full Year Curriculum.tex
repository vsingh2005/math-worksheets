\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 2: Decimal Place Value & Computation ──────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainB}\textbf{Domain: Decimal Place Value \& Computation}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
3 & Decimal Place Value \& Computation & Unit 2: Place Value System & Understanding Powers of 10 &
Recognize that in a multi-digit number, a digit in one place represents 10 times what it represents to its right, and 1/10 to its left. Use exponents. &
5.NBT.A.1 / 5.NBT.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
4 & Decimal Place Value \& Computation & Unit 2: Place Value System & Reading, Writing \& Rounding Decimals &
Read, write, and compare decimals to thousandths using base-ten numerals, number names, and expanded form. Round decimals to any place. &
5.NBT.A.3 / 5.NBT.A.4 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
5 & Decimal Place Value \& Computation & Unit 3: Arithmetic & Whole Number Multiplication &
Fluently multiply multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm. Solve real-world word problems. &
5.NBT.B.5 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
6 & Decimal Place Value \& Computation & Unit 3: Arithmetic & Whole Number Division &
Find whole-number quotients of whole numbers with up to four-digit dividends and two-digit divisors, using equations and rectangular arrays. &
5.NBT.B.6 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
7 & Decimal Place Value \& Computation & Unit 3: Arithmetic & Adding \& Subtracting Decimals &
Add and subtract decimals to hundredths, using concrete models, place value strategies, and written properties. &
5.NBT.B.7 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
8 & Decimal Place Value \& Computation & Unit 3: Arithmetic & Multiplying \& Dividing Decimals &
Multiply and divide decimals to hundredths, using concrete models or drawings. Relate strategies to a written method. &
5.NBT.B.7 \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 3: Fractions ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainC}\textbf{Domain: Fractions}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
9 & Fractions & Unit 4: Fraction Addition \& Subtraction & Adding \& Subtracting Fractions &
Add and subtract fractions and mixed numbers with unlike denominators by replacing them with equivalent fractions with common denominators. &
5.NF.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
10 & Fractions & Unit 4: Fraction Addition \& Subtraction & Fraction Addition/Subtraction Problems &
Solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions referring to the same whole, using visual models and checking for reasonableness. &
5.NF.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
11 & Fractions & Unit 5: Multiplication \& Division & Fractions as Division Word Problems &
Interpret a fraction as division of the numerator by the denominator ($a/b = a \div b$). Solve word problems involving sharing whole items. &
5.NF.B.3 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
12 & Fractions & Unit 5: Multiplication \& Division & Multiplying Fractions & Mixed Numbers &
Multiply fractions or mixed numbers. Find the area of a rectangle with fractional side lengths. Interpret products as scaling. &
5.NF.B.4 / 5.NF.B.5 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
13 & Fractions & Unit 5: Multiplication \& Division & Fraction Multiplication Word Problems &
Solve real-world word problems involving multiplication of fractions and mixed numbers, using visual fraction models and equations. &
5.NF.B.6 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
14 & Fractions & Unit 5: Multiplication \& Division & Dividing Unit Fractions by Wholes &
Apply and extend previous understandings of division to divide unit fractions by non-zero whole numbers (e.g., $1/3 \div 4$). &
5.NF.B.7a / 5.NF.B.7c \\

\rowcolor{row1}
15 & Fractions & Unit 5: Multiplication \& Division & Dividing Wholes by Unit Fractions &
Apply and extend division understandings to divide whole numbers by unit fractions (e.g., $4 \div 1/3$). Solve real-world fraction problems. &
5.NF.B.7b / 5.NF.B.7c \\

% ── DOMAIN 4: Measurement & Data ───────────────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainD}\textbf{Domain: Measurement \& Data}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
16 & Measurement \& Data & Unit 6: Conversions \& Data & Measurement Conversions &
Convert among different-sized standard measurement units within a given measurement system (metric and customary). Solve multi-step problems. &
5.MD.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
17 & Measurement \& Data & Unit 6: Conversions \& Data & Representing Data on Line Plots &
Make a line plot to display a data set of measurements in fractions of a unit ($1/2$, $1/4$, $1/8$). Solve problems using operations on fractions. &
5.MD.B.2 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
18 & Measurement \& Data & Unit 7: Volume Concepts & Understanding Volume &
Recognize volume as an attribute of solid figures and understand concepts of volume measurement. Measure volume by counting unit cubes. &
5.MD.C.3 / 5.MD.C.4 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
19 & Measurement \& Data & Unit 7: Volume Concepts & Volume of Rectangular Prisms &
Relate volume to the operations of multiplication and addition. Find volume of rectangular prisms using $V = lwh$ and $V = Bh$ in word problems. &
5.MD.C.5a-b \\

\rowcolor{row1}
20 & Measurement \& Data & Unit 7: Volume Concepts & Volume of Composite Prisms &
Find volume of solid figures composed of two non-overlapping right rectangular prisms by adding the volumes of the parts. &
5.MD.C.5c \\

21 & Geometry \& Graphs & Unit 8: Coordinate Geometry & Coordinate Plane Intro &

\rowcolor{row1}
22 & Geometry \& Graphs & Unit 8: Coordinate Geometry & Graphing Real-World Problems &
Represent real-world and mathematical problems by graphing points in the first quadrant, and interpret coordinate values of points. &

\rowcolor{row2}
23 & Geometry \& Graphs & Unit 8: Coordinate Geometry & Properties of 2D Figures &

5.G.B.3 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
24 & Geometry \& Graphs & Unit 8: Coordinate Geometry & Classifying Shapes &
Classify two-dimensional figures in a hierarchy based on properties. Group triangles and quadrilaterals based on angles and side relationships. &
5.G.B.4 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
25 & Geometry \& Graphs & Unit 8: Coordinate Geometry & Cumulative Word Problems &
Solve multi-step real-world word problems drawing on all domains: fractions, decimals, volume, coordinate graphing, and geometry. &
5.MP.1--5.MP.8 \\
