\vspace{3mm}
\noindent
To evaluate expressions with parentheses, follow the **Order of Operations** (PEMDAS):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **P**arentheses first: $()$, $[]$, $\{\}$
    \item **E**xponents
    \item **M**ultiplication and **D**ivision (left to right)
    \item **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction (left to right)
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{3mm}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm]
\textbf{Example:}
\[
3 \times (4 + 6) - 5
\]
First, parentheses: $4 + 6 = 10$.
\[
3 \times 10 - 5
\]
Next, multiply: $3 \times 10 = 30$.
\[
30 - 5 = 25
\]
\end{tcolorbox}
