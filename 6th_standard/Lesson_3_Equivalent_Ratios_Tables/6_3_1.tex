\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Equivalent Ratios (Beginning)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
\textbf{Equivalent ratios} represent the same relationship between two quantities. You can create equivalent ratios by multiplying or dividing both parts of a ratio by the same number.

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
% PART A — IDENTIFY EQUIVALENT RATIOS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Which Ratios are Equivalent?}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Circle YES or NO for each pair.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
