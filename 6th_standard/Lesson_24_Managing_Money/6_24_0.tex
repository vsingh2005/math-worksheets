    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Financial Literacy: Budgets, Percents, and Discounts (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{1mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Budgeting:} Balancing your incoming income against outgoing fixed and variable expenses.\\
\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent
2. \textbf{Percentage Discounts \& Increases:} Percentages alter values relative to their immediate base.
   \begin{itemize}[noitemsep, leftmargin=0.5cm]
       \item $\text{Discount Value} = \text{Original Price} \times \text{Rate}_{\text{decimal}}$
       \item $\text{Sale Price} = \text{Original Price} \times (1 - \text{Rate}_{\text{discount\_decimal}})$
       \item $\text{Total Cost with Tax} = \text{Sale Price} \times (1 + \text{Rate}_{\text{tax\_decimal}})$
   \end{itemize}
\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent
3. \textbf{Simple Interest:} Growth modeled linearly over time: $\text{Interest } (I) = P \cdot r \cdot t$.

\vspace{2mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=4mm, bottom=4mm]
\vspace{0.5cm}

\textbf{Worked Example:}
A book is originally priced at $\$20$. It is on sale for $15\%$ off. If the sales tax is $5\%$, calculate the final price.\\
\textbf{Solution:}\\
1) $\text{Discount} = \$20 \times 0.15 = \$3$.\\
2) $\text{Sale Price} = \$20 - \$3 = \$17$.\\
3) $\text{Sales Tax} = \$17 \times 0.05 = \$0.85$.\\
4) $\text{Final Cost} = \$17 + \$0.85 = \$17.85$.
\vspace{0.5cm}
\end{tcolorbox}
