% ==========================================================
% INTRODUCTION — WHAT IS A UNIT RATE?
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Understanding Unit Rates (Day 1)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}

\noindent
A \textbf{unit rate} is a ratio that compares two quantities where one quantity is \textbf{1}. 
For example, if you buy 3 apples for \$6, the unit rate is \$2 per apple. This tells us how much ONE apple costs.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[
    colback=white,
    colframe=black!25,
    boxrule=0.6pt,
    arc=4mm,
    left=6mm,
    right=6mm,
    top=5mm,
    bottom=5mm
]
\textbf{Finding Unit Rates}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=8mm, itemsep=4mm]
    \item \textbf{Write the Ratio:} Start with the relationship between two quantities.
    
    \textbf{Example:} 4 miles in 2 hours $\rightarrow$ $\dfrac{4 \text{ miles}}{2 \text{ hours}}$

    \item \textbf{Divide Both by the Second Quantity:} To find the unit rate, divide both the top and bottom by the bottom number.
    
    $\dfrac{4 \text{ miles}}{2 \text{ hours}} = \dfrac{4 \div 2}{2 \div 2} = \dfrac{2 \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ hour}}$
    
    \item \textbf{Write as a Unit Rate:} The unit rate is \textbf{2 miles per hour} or \textbf{2 mph}.

    \item \textbf{Use Unit Rates to Solve:} If you travel 2 miles per hour, how far in 5 hours?
    
    $2 \text{ miles/hour} \times 5 \text{ hours} = 10 \text{ miles}$
\end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Key Words:} per, each, every hour, per item, miles per hour (mph), cost per item

\noindent
Find the unit rate, then answer the question. Show your work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item A car travels 200 miles in 4 hours. What is the car's speed (miles per hour)? If the car continues at this speed, how far will it travel in 6 hours?\\
\vspace{2cm}

\item At a bakery, 36 cupcakes cost \$18. What is the price per cupcake? How much would 15 cupcakes cost?\\
\vspace{2cm}

\item A runner completes 3 miles in 30 minutes. What is her rate in miles per minute? How far will she run in 50 minutes at this rate?\\
\vspace{2cm}

\item A water pump fills 60 gallons in 5 minutes. What is the pump's rate in gallons per minute? How many gallons will it fill in 12 minutes?\\
\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}
