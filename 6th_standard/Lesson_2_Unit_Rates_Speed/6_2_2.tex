\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Quick Review: Unit Rates}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
A \textbf{unit rate} compares two quantities where the second is \textbf{1}. To find a unit rate, divide both the first quantity and the second quantity by the second quantity.

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
% PART A — UNIT RATES WITH FRACTIONS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Unit Rates with Fractions}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Find each unit rate. Remember: division by a fraction means multiply by its reciprocal.

\vspace{2mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item 8 miles in $\dfrac{1}{2}$ hour\\ \\
Unit rate: \blankshorter \; \textbf{miles per hour}

\item 10 dollars for $\dfrac{2}{3}$ pound\\ \\
Unit rate: \$ \blankshorter \; \textbf{per pound}

\item 15 pages in $\dfrac{3}{4}$ hour\\ \\
Unit rate: \blankshorter \; \textbf{pages per hour}

\item 12 apples in $\dfrac{1}{3}$ bag\\ \\
Unit rate: \blankshorter \; \textbf{apples per bag}

\item $\dfrac{1}{2}$ gallon of paint covers $\dfrac{1}{8}$ wall\\ \\
Unit rate: \blankshorter \; \textbf{gallons per wall}

\end{enumerate}

\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Using Fractions and Unit Rates}}

\noindent

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]
\item A recipe uses 2 cups of rice for $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of oil.

If you use 8 cups of rice, how much oil do you need?

\vspace{2cm}
\item A printer prints 90 pages in $\dfrac{1}{3}$ minute. What is the printing rate in pages per minute? How many pages will it print in $\dfrac{2}{3}$ minute?\\
\vspace{2cm}

\item A cyclist travels 18 miles in $\dfrac{3}{4}$ hour. What is the cyclist's speed in miles per hour? How far will the cyclist travel in $1\dfrac{1}{2}$ hours?\\
\vspace{2cm}


\end{enumerate}
