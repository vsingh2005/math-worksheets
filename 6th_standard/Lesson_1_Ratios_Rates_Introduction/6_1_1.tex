\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=customteal!20]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Apply Ratios}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each problem.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1.8cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A recipe uses 3 cups of flour and 2 cups of sugar. Write the ratio of flour to sugar.

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item A soccer team won 8 games and lost 2 games. Write the ratio of wins to total games.

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item A bookshelf has books in ratio 4 math books to 5 story books. If there are 4 math books, how many story books are there?

\vspace{1.2cm}

\item A classroom has 24 students. The ratio of boys to girls is 3:5. How many boys and how many girls? (Hint: 3 boys for every 5 girls means 8 students per group)

\vspace{1.5cm}

\end{enumerate}
