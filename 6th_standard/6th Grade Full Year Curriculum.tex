% ── DOMAIN 1: Ratios, Rates & Proportions ────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainA}\textbf{Domain: Ratios, Rates \& Proportions}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
1 & Ratios, Rates \& Proportions & Unit 1: Ratios \& Rates & Introduction to Ratios &
Understand the concept of a ratio and use ratio language to describe the relationship between two quantities. Explore part-to-part and part-to-whole ratios with real-world examples. &
6.RP.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
2 & Ratios, Rates \& Proportions & Unit 1: Ratios \& Rates & Unit Rates \& Speed &
Find unit rates associated with ratios of fractions. Solve problems involving unit pricing and constant speed (e.g., miles per hour, cost per item). &
6.RP.A.2 / 6.RP.A.3b \\

\rowcolor{row1}
3 & Ratios, Rates \& Proportions & Unit 1: Ratios \& Rates & Equivalent Ratios \& Ratio Tables &
Make tables of equivalent ratios, find missing values, and plot ratio pairs on the coordinate plane. Use tables to compare ratios. &
6.RP.A.3a \\

\rowcolor{row2}
4 & Ratios, Rates \& Proportions & Unit 2: Percents \& Proportions & Percents as Rates per 100 &
Find a percent of a quantity as a rate per 100. Solve problems finding the whole given a part and percent, and finding a part given the whole and percent. &
6.RP.A.3c \\

\rowcolor{row1}
5 & Ratios, Rates \& Proportions & Unit 2: Percents \& Proportions & Converting Measurement Units &
Use ratio reasoning to convert measurement units (customary and metric). Manipulate and transform units appropriately when multiplying or dividing quantities. &
6.RP.A.3d \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 2: The Number System & Fractions ──────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainB}\textbf{Domain: The Number System \& Fractions}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
6 & The Number System \& Fractions & Unit 3: Fractions \& Decimals & Dividing Fractions by Fractions &
Interpret and compute quotients of fractions. Solve real-world word problems involving division of fractions by fractions using visual models and equations. &
6.NS.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
7 & The Number System \& Fractions & Unit 3: Fractions \& Decimals & Multi-Digit Decimals \& GCF/LCM &
Fluently add, subtract, multiply, and divide multi-digit decimals. Find GCF of two whole numbers $\leq$100 and LCM of two whole numbers $\leq$12; apply the distributive property. &
6.NS.B.2 / 6.NS.B.3 / 6.NS.B.4 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
8 & The Number System \& Fractions & Unit 4: Rational Numbers & Positive \& Negative Numbers &
Understand positive and negative numbers in real-world contexts (temperature, elevation, debits/credits). Represent quantities with positive and negative numbers. &
6.NS.C.5 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
9 & The Number System \& Fractions & Unit 4: Rational Numbers & Number Lines \& Coordinate Plane &
Understand rational numbers as points on a number line. Plot integers and rational numbers on number lines and in all four quadrants of the coordinate plane. &
6.NS.C.6 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
10 & The Number System \& Fractions & Unit 4: Rational Numbers & Ordering \& Absolute Value &
Understand ordering and absolute value of rational numbers. Interpret inequality statements, find absolute value, and distinguish comparisons of absolute value vs.\ order. &
6.NS.C.7 / 6.NS.C.8 \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 3: Expressions & Intro Algebra ────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainC}\textbf{Domain: Expressions \& Intro Algebra}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
11 & Expressions \& Intro Algebra & Unit 5: Algebraic Expressions & Numerical Expressions \& Exponents &
Write and evaluate numerical expressions involving whole-number exponents. Apply order of operations (PEMDAS) to evaluate expressions. &
6.EE.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
12 & Expressions \& Intro Algebra & Unit 5: Algebraic Expressions & Writing \& Evaluating Algebraic Expressions &
Write, read, and evaluate algebraic expressions. Identify parts of an expression (term, factor, coefficient, sum, product). Evaluate expressions by substituting values for variables. &
6.EE.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
13 & Expressions \& Intro Algebra & Unit 5: Algebraic Expressions & Equivalent Expressions \& Properties &
Apply properties of operations (commutative, associative, distributive) to generate equivalent expressions. Factor and expand linear expressions. &
6.EE.A.3 / 6.EE.A.4 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
14 & Expressions \& Intro Algebra & Unit 6: Equations \& Inequalities & Solving One-Variable Equations &
Understand solving an equation as finding values that make it true. Solve one-step equations of the form $x + p = q$ and $px = q$ for non-negative rational numbers. &
6.EE.B.5 / 6.EE.B.7 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
15 & Expressions \& Intro Algebra & Unit 6: Equations \& Inequalities & Inequalities \& Independent/Dependent Variables &
Write and graph inequalities of the form $x > c$ or $x < c$. Analyze relationships between independent and dependent variables using tables and equations (e.g., $d = 65t$). &
6.EE.B.8 / 6.EE.C.9 \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 4: Geometry & Measurement ─────────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainD}\textbf{Domain: Geometry \& Measurement}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
16 & Geometry \& Measurement & Unit 7: Area \& Polygons & Area of Triangles \& Quadrilaterals &
Find area of right triangles, other triangles, and special quadrilaterals by composing into rectangles or decomposing into triangles. Apply to real-world problems. &
6.G.A.1 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
17 & Geometry \& Measurement & Unit 7: Area \& Polygons & Area of Irregular Polygons &
Extend area concepts to irregular polygons by decomposing into triangles and rectangles. Solve complex real-world area problems on the coordinate plane. &
6.G.A.1 / 6.G.A.3 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
18 & Geometry \& Measurement & Unit 8: Surface Area \& Volume & Volume of Rectangular Prisms &
Find the volume of right rectangular prisms with fractional edge lengths using $V = lwh$ and $V = Bh$. Connect to unit cube packing and solve real-world volume problems. &
6.G.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
19 & Geometry \& Measurement & Unit 8: Surface Area \& Volume & Nets \& Surface Area &
Represent 3D figures using nets made of rectangles and triangles. Use nets to find the surface area of prisms and pyramids in real-world and mathematical problems. &
6.G.A.4 \\

\rowcolor{row1}
20 & Geometry \& Measurement & Unit 8: Surface Area \& Volume & Coordinate Geometry \& Measurement Word Problems &
Draw polygons in the coordinate plane given vertex coordinates; use coordinates to find side lengths. Solve multi-step measurement word problems including unit conversion. &
6.G.A.3 / 6.RP.A.3d \\

\midrule
% ── DOMAIN 5: Statistics, Data & Money ───────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{6}{l}{\cellcolor{domainA}\textbf{Domain: Statistics, Data \& Money}} \\
\midrule

\rowcolor{row1}
21 & Statistics, Data \& Money & Unit 9: Statistics \& Data & Statistical Questions \& Data Distributions &
Recognize statistical questions that anticipate variability. Understand that data distributions can be described by their center, spread, and overall shape. &
6.SP.A.1 / 6.SP.A.2 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
22 & Statistics, Data \& Money & Unit 9: Statistics \& Data & Measures of Center: Mean, Median \& Mode &
Calculate mean, median, and mode for data sets. Understand mean as a fair share and balance point. Choose the appropriate measure of center for a given context. &
6.SP.A.3 / 6.SP.B.5c \\

\rowcolor{row1}
23 & Statistics, Data \& Money & Unit 9: Statistics \& Data & Displaying Data: Histograms \& Box Plots &
Display numerical data in plots (dot plots, histograms, box plots). Summarize data sets by reporting observations, measures of center, variability, and overall shape. &
6.SP.B.4 / 6.SP.B.5 \\

\rowcolor{row2}
24 & Statistics, Data \& Money & Unit 10: Financial Literacy \& Money & Managing Money: Budgets, Percents \& Discounts &
Apply percent concepts to financial situations: calculate discounts, sales tax, tips, and simple interest. Create and analyze a personal budget using ratios and percents. &
6.RP.A.3c (applied) \\

\rowcolor{row1}
25 & Statistics, Data \& Money & Unit 10: Financial Literacy \& Money & Cumulative Word Problems \& Real-World Math &
Solve multi-step real-world word problems drawing on all domains: ratios, fractions, expressions, geometry, and data. Practice communicating mathematical reasoning clearly. &
6.MP.1--6.MP.8 \\
