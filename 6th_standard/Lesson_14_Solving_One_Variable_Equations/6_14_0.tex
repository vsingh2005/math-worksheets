% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{One-Step Equations and Solutions (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Equation:} A mathematical statement that two expressions are equal. \\
2. \textbf{Solution:} A specific value of the variable that makes the equation true. We can test if a number is a solution by substituting it. \\
3. \textbf{Inverse Operations:} To solve an equation, perform the opposite operation on both sides to isolate the variable. \\
- Addition $\leftrightarrow$ Subtraction \\
- Multiplication $\leftrightarrow$ Division

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 1:}

\medskip
\noindent
Solve the equation: $\frac{2}{3}x = 8$\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
Multiply both sides by the reciprocal of $\frac{2}{3}$, which is $\frac{3}{2}$:\\
$$x = 8 \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{24}{2} = 12$$
Check: $\frac{2}{3}(12) = 8$. (Correct!)
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
% SECTION 2: PROPERTIES OF EQUALITY
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Properties of Equality (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Addition/Subtraction Property of Equality:} Adding or subtracting the same number from both sides of an equation keeps the equation balanced. \\
2. \textbf{Multiplication/Division Property of Equality:} Multiplying or dividing both sides of an equation by the same non-zero number keeps the equation balanced.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 2:}

\medskip
\noindent
Solve the equation: $x + 4.8 = 11.2$\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Identify the operation on the variable: Addition of 4.8. \\
2) Perform the inverse operation (subtract 4.8 from both sides): \\
$$x + 4.8 - 4.8 = 11.2 - 4.8$$
$$x = 6.4$$
3) Check: $6.4 + 4.8 = 11.2$. (Correct!)
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}
