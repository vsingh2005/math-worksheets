\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Coordinate Geometry and Unit Conversions (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{2mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Coordinate Geometry:} Mapping 2D shapes on a Cartesian grid using ordered pairs $(x, y)$. Calculate side lengths of horizontal or vertical segments by evaluating absolute differences: $d = |x_2 - x_1|$ or $d = |y_2 - y_1|$.\\
2. \textbf{Measurement Conversions:} Transitioning between metrics using conversion factors.
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep, leftmargin=0.5cm]
    \item \textbf{Customary Linear:} $1\text{ foot (ft)} = 12\text{ inches (in)}$, $1\text{ yard (yd)} = 3\text{ feet}$, $1\text{ mile} = 5280\text{ feet}$.
    \item \textbf{Metric Linear:} $1\text{ meter (m)} = 100\text{ centimeters (cm)} = 1000\text{ millimeters (mm)}$, $1\text{ kilometer (km)} = 1000\text{ m}$.
    \item \textbf{Volume \& Flow Capacity:} $1\text{ cubic meter (m}^3) = 1000\text{ liters (L)}$. $\text{Volume} = \text{Base Area} \times \text{Depth}$.
\end{itemize}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=4mm, bottom=4mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}
Find the perimeter of a rectangle with horizontal vertices at $(1, 2)$ and $(5, 2)$. If each grid unit represents 3 inches, find the length of this side in inches.\\
\textbf{Solution:}
$$\text{Grid Distance} = 5 - 1 = 4\text{ units.}$$
$$\text{Actual Length} = 4\text{ units} \times 3\text{ inches/unit} = 12\text{ inches.}$$
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black, boxrule=1pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Deeper Thinking Problem 1: Grid Map Planning}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{a)} A rectangular park has vertices at $A(-3, -2)$, $B(5, -2)$, $C(5, 4)$, and $D(-3, 4)$. If each unit on the grid represents 5 yards, find the actual dimensions, perimeter, and area of the park. Show your conversion steps clearly. Draw out a diagram to support your answer.
\vspace{8cm}

\noindent
\textbf{b)} A triangular garden has vertices $X(1, 2)$, $Y(7, 2)$, and $Z(1, 6)$. Find the area of the garden in grid square units. If 1 grid square unit = 4 square feet, what is the area of the garden in square feet? Draw out a diagram to support your answer.
\vspace{8cm}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black, boxrule=1pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Deeper Thinking Problem 2: Conversion Multipliers}
\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{a)} Explain how to convert an area of 5 square yards into square feet. (Hint: Draw or conceptualize a $1\text{ yd} \times 1\text{ yd}$ square block broken into feet.)
\vspace{4cm}

\noindent
\textbf{b)} A structural monitoring sensor records a displacement of 3 kilometers. Express this distance in centimeters. Show each conversion factor cascade explicitly.
\vspace{3cm}

\noindent
\textbf{c)} A swimming pool has a volume of 2 cubic yards. Convert this volume into cubic feet. (Hint: Think about how many cubic feet fit along each edge of a $1\text{ yd} \times 1\text{ yd} \times 1\text{ yd}$ cube.)
\vspace{4cm}

\noindent
\textbf{d)} A car travels at a speed of 60 miles per hour. Convert this speed into feet per second by cascading two separate unit conversions, one for distance and one for time, and show each step explicitly.
\vspace{3cm}

\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black, boxrule=1pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Deeper Thinking Problem 3: Practical Coordinate Design}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{a)} A storage unit blueprint is drawn on a coordinate plane. The rectangular floor has vertices at $W(-6, -4)$, $X(4, -4)$, $Y(4, 3)$, and $Z(-6, 3)$. Each grid unit represents $1.5$ feet. Calculate the actual perimeter of the floor in feet.
\vspace{5cm}

\noindent
\textbf{b)} Calculate the actual floor area of the storage unit in square feet. Convert this value into square inches. ($1\text{ square foot} = 144\text{ square inches}$)
\vspace{5cm}

\noindent
\textbf{c)} A secondary shelf is placed at the corner, modeled by a triangle with vertices at $(-6, 3)$, $(-2, 3)$, and $(-6, -1)$. Calculate the shelf's grid area and actual area in square feet.
\vspace{5cm}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black, boxrule=1pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Deeper Thinking Problem 4: Multi-Step Metric Mapping}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{a)} A hiking path is mapped with coordinates. Vertex $M(-8, 5)$ represents the starting point, and $N(7, 5)$ represents a scenic overlook. If each unit represents $0.2$ kilometers, how far is the overlook from the start in meters?
\vspace{5cm}

\noindent
\textbf{b)} The path then goes from $N(7, 5)$ to $P(7, -3)$. Calculate the total length of the path $M \rightarrow N \rightarrow P$ in grid units, then convert this total distance into centimeters.
\vspace{5cm}

\noindent
\textbf{c)} A backpacker walks this entire route ($M \rightarrow N \rightarrow P$) in $45$ minutes. What is their average speed in kilometers per hour?
\vspace{5cm}
