% ==========================================================
% PART A — FORMULATING AND ANALYZING INEQUALITIES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Formulating and Analyzing Inequalities}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Write inequalities and solve to answer each question.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1.5cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A local theater budget limits expenses to a maximum of \$1,200 per show. \\ \\
\textbf{a)} Write an inequality to represent this budget limit $B$. \blanklong \\ \\
\textbf{b)} If a performance costs \$950 to set up plus \$50 per hour of rehearsal $h$, write an inequality for the rehearsal hours. \blanklong \\ \\
\textbf{c)} Solve for the maximum hours of rehearsal they can afford.

\vspace{1cm}

\item A rental company charges a \$20 base fee plus \$5 per hour to rent a bicycle. \\ \\
\textbf{a)} State the independent and dependent variables. \blanklong \\ \\
\textbf{b)} Write an equation for the total cost $C$ in terms of hours $h$. \blanklong \\ \\
\textbf{c)} If you have \$45, write an inequality to show the maximum hours you can rent the bicycle. Solve it.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A student has a gift card with \$50. They want to buy songs online that cost \$1.25 each. Write an inequality representing the number of songs $s$ they can buy. Solve it.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A school fundraiser must raise \textbf{at least} \$500 to fund a field trip. The class has already raised \$180, and each raffle ticket sells for \$4. \\ \\
\textbf{a)} Write an inequality representing the number of raffle tickets $t$ they still need to sell. \\ \\ \blanklong \\ \\
\textbf{b)} Solve the inequality and interpret your answer in a complete sentence.

\vspace{2cm}

\item A delivery driver earns \$15 per hour and receives a one-time \$30 bonus for working a weekend shift. \\ \\
\textbf{a)} Write an equation for total earnings $E$ in terms of hours worked $h$ on a weekend shift. \\ \\ \blanklong \\ \\
\textbf{b)} Write an inequality for the number of hours the driver must work to earn \textbf{more than} \$120. Solve it.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART B — GRAPHING AND CHECKING INEQUALITIES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Graphing and Checking Inequalities}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve the conceptual graphing problems. Show all work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item Write an inequality for ``values of $y$ that are strictly greater than $-4$ but no more than 6.''

\vspace{2cm}

\item Write an inequality representing a number line graph with an open circle at $-2.5$ and an arrow pointing to the right. Is $-2$ a solution? Is $-3$ a solution?

\vspace{2cm}

\item If $x \le y$ and $y < z$, what can you say about the relationship between $x$ and $z$? Write an inequality comparing $x$ and $z$, and explain.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART C — ANALYZING VARIABLE RELATIONSHIPS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Analyzing Variable Relationships}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Analyze rate equations and independent/dependent variables.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A car rental agency charges a flat rate of \$40 plus \$0.20 per mile driven $m$. Write an equation for the total cost $C$. If the total cost is plotted on a graph, which axis represents miles and which represents cost?

\vspace{2cm}

\item A swimming pool is being filled. The volume $V$ of water after $t$ minutes is given by $V = 100 + 15t$. If you double the filling rate, write a new equation and compare the volume of both pools after 10 minutes.

\vspace{2cm}

\item Identify the independent and dependent variables in the relationship: ``The amount of money earned by a worker is related to the number of hours worked.'' Explain your reasoning.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Challenge Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve the advanced inequality problems. Show all work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.4cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item Find all integer solutions that satisfy both $x \ge -2$ and $x < 3$.

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A store has a policy: ``Spend at least \$75 to get free shipping.'' A customer buys 3 items that cost $x$ dollars each. Write an inequality representing the value of $x$ required to get free shipping. Solve it.

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A candle starts at a height of 12 inches and burns at a rate of 1.5 inches per hour. Write an equation representing the height $H$ after $t$ hours. Write an inequality representing the hours $t$ for which the candle's height is at least 3 inches, and solve it.

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}
