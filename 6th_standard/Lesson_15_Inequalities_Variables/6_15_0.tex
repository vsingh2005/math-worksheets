\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Inequalities and Relationships (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Inequality:} A mathematical statement showing that two quantities are not equal ($<$, $>$, $\le$, $\ge$). \\
- An inequality like $x > c$ represents infinitely many solutions. \\
- \textbf{Graphing:} Use an open circle for $<$ or $>$, and a closed circle for $\le$ or $\ge$. \\
2. \textbf{Variables:} \\
- \textbf{Independent Variable:} The input variable that causes a change in another variable (usually $x$). \\
- \textbf{Dependent Variable:} The output variable that changes in response to the independent variable (usually $y$).

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 1: Writing and Graphing}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Problem:} Write and graph the inequality: ``The temperature $T$ must be under $-5^\circ\text{C}$''.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Identify the inequality symbol: ``under'' means strictly less than ($<$). \\
2) Write the inequality: $T < -5$. \\
3) Graph the solution: \\
- Draw an open circle at $-5$ (since $-5$ is not included). \\
- Draw an arrow pointing to the left (representing all numbers less than $-5$).
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 1B: Solving Multi-Step Inequalities}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Instruction:} Solving an inequality is just like solving an equation: perform the same inverse operations on both sides to isolate the variable. 

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Problem:} Solve the inequality $3x + 15 \le 45$ and describe its graph.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Subtract 15 from both sides: $3x \le 30$. \\
2) Divide both sides by 3: $x \le 10$. \\
3) Graph: A closed circle at 10 with an arrow pointing to the left.
\end{tcolorbox}

% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Variables in Tables and Graphs (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Equations with Two Variables:} Write equations to show how the dependent variable $y$ changes with the independent variable $x$. \\
2. \textbf{Tables:} Columns show the values of the independent variable and the calculated values of the dependent variable. \\
3. \textbf{Graphs:} The independent variable is always plotted on the horizontal axis ($x$-axis), and the dependent variable is on the vertical axis ($y$-axis).\\
4. \textbf{Rates of Change:} Equations can model increasing relationships ($y = mx + b$) or decreasing relationships ($y = b - mx$, such as quantities draining, losing weight, or melting).

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 2: Table to Equation}

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Problem:} A painter charges \$15 per hour. Create an equation for the total cost $C$ after $h$ hours. Find the cost for 4 hours.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Identify variables: Hours $h$ is independent; total cost $C$ is dependent. \\
2) Write the equation: $C = 15h$. \\
3) Evaluate for $h = 4$: $C = 15(4) = 60$. \\
4) Answer: The total cost for 4 hours is \$60.
\end{tcolorbox}
