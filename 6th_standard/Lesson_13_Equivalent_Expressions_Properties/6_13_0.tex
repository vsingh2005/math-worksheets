% SECTION 1: ALGEBRAIC PROPERTIES AND EQUIVALENCY
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Algebraic Properties and Equivalency (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Properties:} \\
- \textbf{Commutative Property:} $a + b = b + a$, \quad $ab = ba$ \\
- \textbf{Associative Property:} $(a+b)+c = a+(b+c)$, \quad $(ab)c = a(bc)$ \\
- \textbf{Distributive Property:} $a(b+c) = ab + ac$, \quad $ab + ac = a(b+c)$ \\
2. \textbf{Combining Like Terms:} Add or subtract coefficients of terms with the same variable. Example: $3x + 5 + 2x - 1 = 5x + 4$.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 1:}

\medskip
\noindent
Expand the expression $4(2x + 3)$ using the Distributive Property, then check equivalence by substituting $x=2$.\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Expand: $4 \times 2x + 4 \times 3 = 8x + 12$. \\
2) Check for $x=2$: \\
- Original: $4(2(2) + 3) = 4(4 + 3) = 4(7) = 28$. \\
- Expanded: $8(2) + 12 = 16 + 12 = 28$. \\
- Since both equal 28, the expressions are equivalent.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Combining Like Terms and Factoring (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Like Terms:} Terms that have the same variables raised to the same power. Constant numbers are also like terms. \\
2. \textbf{Factoring (GCF):} The opposite of distributing. Find the Greatest Common Factor of all terms and place it outside parentheses.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example 2:}

\medskip
\noindent
Factor the expression: $15m + 10$\\
\textbf{Solution:} \\
1) Find the GCF of the terms $15m$ and $10$. The GCF of 15 and 10 is 5. \\
2) Divide each term by the GCF: \\
- $15m \div 5 = 3m$ \\
- $10 \div 5 = 2$ \\
3) Rewrite as a product: $5(3m + 2)$.
\end{tcolorbox}
