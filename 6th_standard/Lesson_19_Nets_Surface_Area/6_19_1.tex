% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Surface Area Word Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve. Show all steps and include units.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.4cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A gift box is a rectangular prism with dimensions $20\text{ cm} \times 15\text{ cm} \times 8\text{ cm}$. How much wrapping paper is needed to cover all six faces?

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A paint crew needs to paint the outside of a shed (all 4 walls and the roof). The shed is $10\text{ ft} \times 8\text{ ft}$ and $6\text{ ft}$ tall. The roof is also a rectangular prism top: $10\text{ ft} \times 8\text{ ft}$. How many square feet of total surface will be painted? (Ignore the floor.)

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A toy company makes cubes with side length 4 in. They paint all faces of each cube. If one can of paint covers 200 square inches, how many cubes can be painted with one can?

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}
