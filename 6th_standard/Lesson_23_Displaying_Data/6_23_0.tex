\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
    \large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Histograms and Box-and-Whisker Plots (Foundation)}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent
1. \textbf{Histogram:} A bar graph where data is grouped into intervals (bins) of equal size. Bins must not overlap. The height of each bar represents the frequency. \\
2. \textbf{Box Plot (Box-and-Whisker):} Summarizes data using a 5-number summary: \\
- \textbf{Minimum:} The smallest value. \\
- \textbf{Lower Quartile ($Q_1$):} The median of the lower half of data. \\
- \textbf{Median ($Q_2$):} The middle value of the data. \\
- \textbf{Upper Quartile ($Q_3$):} The median of the upper half of data. \\
- \textbf{Maximum:} The largest value. \\
3. \textbf{Interquartile Range (IQR):} $Q_3 - Q_1$ (represents the middle $50\%$ of the data).

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!25, boxrule=0.6pt, arc=4mm, left=6mm, right=6mm, top=5mm, bottom=5mm]
\textbf{Worked Example:}

\medskip
\noindent
Find the 5-number summary of: $2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10$.\\
\textbf{Solution:}\\
- Median ($Q_2$) is the middle value: $6$.\\
- Lower half is $\{2, 4, 5\}$, so $Q_1 = 4$.\\
- Upper half is $\{8, 9, 10\}$, so $Q_3 = 9$.\\
- Min $= 2$, Max $= 10$. IQR $= 9 - 4 = 5$.
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{4mm}
