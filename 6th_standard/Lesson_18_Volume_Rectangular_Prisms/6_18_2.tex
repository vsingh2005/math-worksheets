\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Using the $B \times h$ Formula}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve for the missing value. Show your equation and steps.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item $B = 24\text{ cm}^2$, $h = 5\text{ cm}$. Find $V$.

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item $B = 45\text{ in}^2$, $h = 2.2\text{ in}$. Find $V$.

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item $V = 60\text{ m}^3$, $B = 15\text{ m}^2$. Find $h$.

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item $V = 120\text{ ft}^3$, $h = 8\text{ ft}$. Find $B$.

\vspace{1.8cm}

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART B — WORD PROBLEMS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Volume Word Problems}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each problem. Show all steps.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A sandbox is 6 feet long, 4.5 feet wide, and 1.2 feet deep. How many cubic feet of sand does it hold when filled?

\vspace{3cm}

\item A shipping box has a volume of 480 cubic inches. If the base area is 80 square inches, what is its height?

\vspace{3cm}

\item A cereal box is $8\text{ in}$ tall, $3\text{ in}$ wide, and $1.5\text{ in}$ deep. What is its volume?

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART C — FRACTIONAL DIMENSIONS AND PACKING
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Fractional Dimensions and Cube Packing}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each problem. Express answers as fractions or decimals.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.3cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A box is $2\frac{1}{2}$ ft long, $1\frac{1}{2}$ ft wide, and $\frac{3}{4}$ ft tall. What is its volume?

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A rectangular prism is $\frac{3}{4}$ m long, $\frac{2}{3}$ m wide, and $\frac{1}{2}$ m tall. Find its volume as a simplified fraction.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A box with volume $\frac{1}{8}$ ft$^3$ needs to be packed with cubes that each have side length $\frac{1}{4}$ ft. How many cubes fit inside?

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}

% ==========================================================
% PART D — CHALLENGE PROBLEMS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Challenge: Multi-Step Volume}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Reason through each complex scenario. Show all work.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.4cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item A swimming pool is 20 meters long, 8 meters wide, and 2 meters deep. How much water (in cubic meters) is needed to fill it? If water costs \$0.05 per cubic meter, what is the total cost?

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item A large moving truck's cargo hold is 12 ft long, 7 ft wide, and 6 ft tall. A customer wants to fill it with boxes that are 3 ft $\times$ 3.5 ft $\times$ 2 ft each. What is the maximum number of boxes that fit?

\vspace{2.3cm}

\item Two rectangular prisms have the same volume of 360 cm$^3$. Prism A has dimensions $10 \times 6 \times h$. Prism B has dimensions $15 \times 4 \times h$. Find the height of each prism. Which prism is taller?

\vspace{2.5cm}

\end{enumerate}
