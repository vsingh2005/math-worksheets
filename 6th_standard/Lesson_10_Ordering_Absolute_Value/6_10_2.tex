\noindent
Solve the ordering scenarios below.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item Three cities have recorded temperatures of $-5^\circ\text{F}$, $-12^\circ\text{F}$, and $2^\circ\text{F}$. Order these cities from coldest to warmest.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item Three submarines are at elevations of $-120$ meters, $-350$ meters, and $-80$ meters relative to sea level. Order these submarines from deepest to shallowest.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item Three hikers start from sea level. Hiker A goes to an altitude of $-10$ feet, Hiker B goes to $-25$ feet, and Hiker C goes to $-5$ feet. Order them from lowest to highest altitude.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\end{enumerate}
