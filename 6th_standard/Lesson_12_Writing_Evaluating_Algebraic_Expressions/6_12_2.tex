Develop your skills in identifying parts of algebraic expressions, modeling real-world situations, and evaluating formulas.

\vspace{4mm}

% ==========================================================
% PART A — REAL-WORLD ALGEBRAIC WRITING
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Real-World Algebraic Writing}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Write an algebraic expression for each situation described below.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item The cost of $p$ movie tickets at \$12 each. \\
Expression: \blanklong

\vspace{2cm}

\item The total cost of 3 pizzas at $d$ dollars each plus a \$5 delivery tip. \\
Expression: \blanklong

\vspace{2cm}

\item The remaining amount of money from a \$50 bill after buying $b$ books for \$6 each. \\
Expression: \blanklong

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART B — EXPRESSION VOCABULARY IDENTIFICATION
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Expression Vocabulary Identification}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Use the expression $4x + 3y - 7$ to answer the following questions.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item Identify the number of terms and list each term in the expression.

\vspace{2cm}

\item Identify the coefficients of the variables $x$ and $y$.

\vspace{2cm}

\item Identify the constant term in the expression.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART C — EVALUATING WITH MULTIPLE VARIABLES
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Evaluating with Multiple Variables}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Evaluate each expression for the given variable values. Show your calculations.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item Evaluate: $3m - 2n$ \quad for \ $m = 5$ \ and \ $n = 4$.

\vspace{2cm}

\item Evaluate: $a^2 + 2b - 3$ \quad for \ $a = 3$ \ and \ $b = 7$.

\vspace{2cm}

\item Evaluate: $\frac{x + y}{z}$ \quad for \ $x = 10$, \ $y = 8$, \ and \ $z = 3$.

\vspace{2cm}

\end{enumerate}

\newpage

% ==========================================================
% PART D — GEOMETRIC AND PRACTICAL FORMULAS
% ==========================================================
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=black!10]
\large\textcolor{black}{\textbf{Geometric and Practical Formulas}}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
Solve each word problem using algebraic expressions.

\vspace{3mm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.2cm, leftmargin=0.6cm]

\item The perimeter of a rectangle is given by the expression $2l + 2w$. Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length $l = 15$ cm and width $w = 8$ cm.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item A plumber charges a flat fee of \$50 plus \$30 per hour of labor. Write an algebraic expression for the total charge after $h$ hours of work.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\item Using your expression from the previous problem, calculate the total charge for a plumbing job that takes $3.5$ hours.

\vspace{2.2cm}

\end{enumerate}
